# Batch summary

## Recurring patterns
- **Mesh everywhere (16/20 images):** sleeves (long or 3/4), yokes with **sweetheart edges** (SOZ AA3, Ella navy and black, videoframe, Treille lining), shoulder wedges, side waist windows (SOZ, videoframe) and midriff bands (Mae, Treille). Skin visibility runs from about 30% to 50%, and the mesh tint varies (black, navy, grey, ocean blue, violet).
- **Inner lining visible through mesh:** Treille (all four images) and Sparkle Dream Mae show an opaque crop/bandeau shape under a mesh shell, with a mesh-only midriff band below it. This is a distinct two-layer look.
- **Horizontal or chevron waist seam with a decorated border band:** nearly every design. The border can be a straight multi-row band (Mae, 220432, Instagram), a chevron (080500, Zalia, SOZ back), a scalloped lace band (080718/749) or a fringe-drop border (Treille).
- **Radiating/converging stone lines:** sunburst/starburst (SOZ front and back), converging fan (Instagram), radial rays (080500), fan rows (Zalia), converging S-curves (videoframe).
- **Outline-drawn motifs:** lotus/mandala (Ella), scroll/swirl vines (Tara), loop/petal plus rosettes (080718). All are single-row stone polylines following vector curves, mirrored about centre front.
- **Mixed stone shapes and palettes:** navettes (080500, Mae, Treille), coloured stones (turquoise in Mae, amethyst in Zalia, rose/pink in Ella black and Tara, gold in Ella navy, mixed rose/violet/blue in videoframe).
- **Density gradients:** fade from a seam downward (080500, videoframe), shoulder-cap → elbow on sleeves (Treille Ocean, Mae, SOZ), increasing toward star tips (SOZ).
- **Back closures and hardware:** hook-and-eye (SOZ), clear plastic hook (Mae, Ella), metal hook-and-bar (Tara), invisible zip (Treille). Hardware is a visible design element in 6 images.
- **Shiny fabrics:** mystique (080500, Ella ×3, Mae), wet-look/metallic foil (Tara aubergine, Mae lower back, Instagram royal blue, Zalia coral).

## Rare or unique features in this batch
- **SOZLEO2535 back:** a circular open cut-out with a **multi-strap double-X lattice** (4–6 straps crossing) and contact shadows on the skin. This is the most complex strap system.
- **Ella black back:** a **6-strap fan** hanging from the neck band across an open U, then over mesh, anchored to a sweetheart back panel edge.
- **Tara back:** a **ladder keyhole**, a tall slot plus two smaller openings separated by bound bars, with scalloped/convex sides.
- **Mae back:** a teardrop keyhole with a clear plastic clasp.
- **Patterned (lace-print) mesh** (videoframe), NEW.
- **Fringe-drop stone border** (Treille), NEW.
- **Scalloped panel edge** along the armhole (Ella).
- **Print-only faux panelling** with printed outlines and gradients (220432).
- **Bound contrast strips framing every seam,** plus a two-band chevron with a third-colour gap (080500).
- **Ombre sublimation across a whole front panel** (SOZ AA3).
- **Colourway pair:** the same design in two palettes, fabrics and stones both swapped (Ella navy/gold vs black/rose; Treille grey vs ocean, which also changes silhouette).

## Top 10 capabilities this batch demands
1. **Mesh material system:** tint colour, opacity/transmission over the skin render, weave normal, an optional **pattern texture** (lace print), and an **optional inner lining layer** with its own outline (bandeau or crop top) showing through.
2. **Free-form panel editor:** arbitrary curved and straight boundaries (sweetheart, chevron, X-cross with fan-out, gothic arch, T/anchor, kite side windows, scalloped edges). Panels can wrap from front to back across the side seams, have mirror-symmetry toggles and support ombre/gradient fills.
3. **Cut-out and strap system:** arbitrary closed openings (circle, teardrop keyhole, U, ladder slots with bars) with bound edges. A **strap generator** with anchor points on necklines, panel edges and side seams supports fan, cross and lattice layouts, layering over openings and mesh, a per-strap stone row, and **contact shadows/AO onto the skin**.
4. **Stone path tools:** stones along any curve at a set pitch; **N parallel offset rows**; rows offset from a panel or print edge; automatic outline of a panel boundary; spiral/scroll curve primitives; and path stones that wrap around sleeves.
5. **Pattern generators for stones:** **starburst/sunray** (rays, length, density and size gradient), **converging/diverging line families**, **diamond lattice/trellis with cell motifs**, **concentric scallop/mandala arcs**, and **border bands** (multi-row, scalloped rosette, **fringe drops**). All are clipped to panel bounds and laid out in garment space rather than UV.
6. **Density-gradient scatter:** uniform, grid-jittered or random scatter with falloff controlled by distance from a seam or point, or along an axis (for example shoulder cap → elbow).
7. **Motif library plus SVG import:** stamp reusable motifs (5-stone plus, rosette, 4-point navette star, asterisk, lotus, snowflake). Import vector outlines and convert them to stone polylines. Convert text or monograms to stones. Mirror placement.
8. **Stone variety and rendering:** round sizes ss6–ss30, **navette (marquise) shape with orientation control** (radial or tangent), coloured stones and **AB coatings** (angle-dependent pink/green/blue fire), per-path multi-colour palettes, pinpoint glints, and an occasional starburst flare.
9. **Fabric shaders:** mystique (micro-dot metallic and glitter grain), wet-look/metallic foil (sharp broad specular on convex forms), satin/matte tricot, glitter tricot with a coloured sparkle base, plus sublimation print layers (vector polygons, gradients, constant-width outline strokes).
10. **Trims and hardware:** contrast binding on necklines, armholes and openings; **bound contrast strips along seams**; multi-stripe bands; mock-neck and halter silhouettes; raglan and set-in sleeves (long, 3/4); cuff bands; invisible zip; hook-and-eye, metal hook-and-bar and clear plastic clasps; embroidered and printed logo decals. Also **colourway swapping** that re-maps fabric and stone palettes in one action.
