# Part summary

- **Mesh yokes** in 12 of 20, usually continuing over the shoulders into the sleeve caps; lower edges a
  shallow V with peak, sweetheart, deep V, straight or asymmetric diagonal; opaque sweetheart edge
  often visible under the mesh.
- **Stone lines are the main design language:** rain/chandelier lines, sunburst rays, lattices,
  garlands, curve families, almost always mirrored; mostly AB/clear small-medium mixed sizes.
- **Edge-anchored density gradients** in ~15 of 20; seam outlines single, double or one-sided bands;
  cuff-weighted sleeve gradients; side waist mesh insets (lens or hourglass); waist chevrons to the
  navel; raglan sleeves with piped raglan lines.
- **Backs:** all three back views open (round keyhole, teardrop V, tombstone + ladder), each with a CB
  neck closure.
- **Unique here:** printed lace on nude mesh (border down a sleeve, body edge cut to the lace outline,
  different laces per region); velvet mandarin collar with piped keyhole; binding-strip cage keyhole in a
  yoke; crossing strips over nude side insets; AB → sapphire stone ombre; lattice with rosettes;
  asymmetric mesh/opaque sleeves; chameleon foil leopard; pearl liquid hologram; ladder back with
  filled cells; front/back split at different seam heights; star-filter flares in brand I photos.
- **Photography:** groups A-H light-grey seamless soft key, AB fire, no flares; brand I high-key white
  with added starbursts; group J indoor ambient with mirror-like wet-look highlights; shiny fabric
  bridges flat across the cleavage.
