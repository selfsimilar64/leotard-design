# Takeaway

The space between **brand configurators** (recolour a fixed template zone by zone; see
[brand design studios](/domain/market/brand-design-studios.md)) and **professional fashion CAD**
(pattern-first, cloth simulation, 4-8 weeks to learn; see
[professional fashion CAD](/domain/market/professional-fashion-cad.md)) appears unoccupied for
leotards. No tool examined lets a user draw a panel line or place a single stone, and no startup,
app or research project dedicated to 3D or AI-assisted leotard, dancewear or skating-dress design
was found.[^notes-tools][^report]

**Caveat: provisional.** It rests on a single search pass cut short when the shared search budget
ran out; academic literature was not searched at all, and app stores and Roblox were not searched
for children's leotard-design games, which likely exist.[^notes-tools] Absence of evidence here is
not evidence of absence.

A German review stresses CLO is "im Kern kein KI-Tool" (at its core not an AI tool) but physics
simulation and CAD.[^ki-syndikat]

# Generative AI reception

inZOI drew negative user reviews in May 2025 after announcing generative AI use, while its AI
pattern/object sharing platform passed 470,000 uploads.[^inzoi] The Dressmaker prototype page
labels itself "No generative AI was used".[^dressmaker-itch]

- **[INFERRED]** Creative audiences are split: B2B platforms adopt AI, game audiences punish it, a
  hit game advertises its absence. Any AI help (suggest a crystal fill or colourway) is safer
  opt-in and assistive than as the headline.[^notes-tools]

# What the gap is, precisely

- **[INFERRED]** Free-form panel design and direct crystal placement (single stones and brushes),
  in real time, with convincing rendering.[^notes-tools] The existing tools compete on
  convenience and ordering integration, not creative freedom.
- **[INFERRED]** Hobbyist assets and flat templates show people already try to visualise leotard
  designs with whatever exists: weak but consistent evidence of latent interest. See
  [demand evidence](/domain/market/demand-evidence.md).
