# Takeaway

> Written by the garment/rules/sizing agent from the styles research note only; the market section
> of this bundle ([/domain/market/](/domain/market/about.md)) may hold overlapping material from
> the existing-tools note. Merge rather than duplicate.

The market runs from about **USD 20-60 training tanks**, through **USD 100-380 made-to-order club
competition leotards** (up to about **USD 700** with heavy Swarovski coverage), to an estimated
**USD 1,000-5,000 Olympic piece**. GK Elite (US) is the largest and outfits Team USA; Sylvia P
(Australia) and Quatro (UK) are the main international custom houses with online 3D or kit-builder
tools; Snowflake, Plum and Destira serve US clubs at lower prices. Milano Pro Sport (UK) entered
liquidation in July 2026.[^styles] Prices are list prices seen 2026-10-13.

# Inferences from the note [INFERRED]

- GK special-order prices look like base prices (crystal patterns optional), so crystalled GK team
  leotards cost more than USD 178-378.
- Price is driven by sleeve length (tank vs sleeved roughly triples the median at Sylvia P), crystal
  brand and coverage, and recolour-vs-bespoke; a live estimate from those inputs would match how
  brands quote.
- Existing 3D tools (GK UDesign, Sylvia P Studio SP, Quatro's kit builder) are recolour-and-embellish
  configurators over fixed styles, not free-form pattern design: the gap a professional tool would
  fill.
- Milano's exit shifts UK club demand to Quatro and overseas brands.[^styles]

**Gaps:** no market-share or revenue data; no price for fully bespoke heavy-Swarovski GK team
leotards or NCAA contracts; brands not examined include Agiva, Elegant Sports, Rebecca's Mom,
Garland, Under Armour/adidas team programmes.[^styles]
