# Takeaway

The default modern display mannequin is **fibreglass with a matte white finish** and an **abstract
egg (oval) head** or no head. It stands on a **round tempered-glass base plate, about 16 in (41 cm)
across for adults and about 13¾ in (35 cm) for children**, with a steel rod rising from the plate
into the **calf** (sometimes also a spigot into the foot). Adult display mannequins are about **180
cm tall with an 86-61-91 cm figure**, much taller and slimmer than real women.[^fit]

# Appearance

| Feature | Facts |
|---|---|
| Head | Abstract heads common; the same body often sold with oval, abstract or no head; some ship an egg head plus an optional rotating semi-abstract head[^archiexpo-heads]. The Eve line is an "Abstract Female Mannequin with Egg Head"[^fd-eve]. Mannequins became abstract and lost facial features in the 1970s; semi-abstract heads are returning[^fashionunited] |
| Finish | Matte white most common ("Matte True White"); also white high gloss and matte grey, sometimes scratch-resistant; skin tones (matte or metallic such as bronze) about €150 extra; usually fibreglass-reinforced polyester (FRP)[^archiexpo-finish]. Best Mannequins Atlantic egg-head: matte "milk white"[^archiexpo-atlantic]. Cheap polypropylene mannequins come in black and colours[^walmart] |
| Hands | "Realistic", "slightly detailed fingers" or articulated; often detachable or twist-lock at the wrist for dressing[^fd-gene]. Econoco City Kid: "realistic hand and foot style"[^citykid12] |
| Hands and feet on abstract lines **[INFERRED]** | Fingers sculpted together with a separate thumb, no nails; smooth shoe-less "Barbie" foot, often slightly arched, no or lightly suggested toes[^fit] |
| Pose | Early mannequins: right foot forward, left foot forward, or feet together; makers release 6-12 new poses per collection[^fashionunited]. Lines sold as numbered poses ("Pose 1 of 6") with variants such as right leg bent, left leg forward, hand on hip[^econoco-amber][^citykid12] |

# Adult proportions

A typical female display mannequin: **5 ft 11 in (180 cm), dress size 4-6, 34B bust, 24-25 in
(61-64 cm) waist, 36 in (91 cm) hips**, about 6 in taller than the average woman.[^fashionunited]
Another common figure: about 6 ft, 34-24-34 in (86-61-86 cm). Retailers say thinner mannequins are
easier to dress and clothes hang better on them.[^refinery29] See
[real-body vs retail mannequin](/domain/mannequins/real-body-vs-retail-mannequin.md).

# Suggested 3D presets [INFERRED]

Matte white (roughness ~0.6-0.7, warm-white albedo ~#EDEAE4) default; gloss white (roughness ~0.15,
clearcoat) and matte black alternatives. Featureless egg head about 22 cm tall (adult) plus a
headless toggle with a flat neck cap; do not use a modelled face. Mitten hands with separate thumb;
smooth toeless feet. Near-A-pose: arms 10-15° out and slightly bent, weight on one leg, other knee
slightly forward, keeping side seams and armholes visible. Clear/tinted glass disc Ø 40.6 × 0.8 cm
(adult and teen), Ø 35 cm (child), chrome Ø ~1.2 cm rod into the back of the calf about 30 cm
up.[^fit]
