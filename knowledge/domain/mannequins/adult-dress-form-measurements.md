# Takeaway

An adult size 4-6 dress form measures about **86-90 / 67-72 / 92-95 cm** (bust/waist/hip).[^fit]
Brand sizing varies widely: a "size 8" waist ranges from 67 to 75 cm across these sources.[^greneker]

| Source | Size | Bust | Waist | Hip | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shop Co soft-foam 3/4 body form (ASTM regular)[^shopco-adult] | 4 | 86.4 | 69.9 | 92.1 | neck 35.6, across shoulder 38.4, thigh 53.8, back length 40.9, front 36.1, total rise 63.5 |
| same | 6 | 89.5 | 72.4 | 95.3 | neck 36.1, shoulder 38.6, thigh 57.7, back 40.9, front 36.3, rise 64.0 |
| same | 8 | 92.1 | 74.9 | 97.8 | neck 37.1, shoulder 39.4, back 40.9 (thigh value looks like a typo) |
| Mood Fabrics chart (snippet)[^mood] | 8 | 90.2 | 67.3 | 92.7 | neck 34.3, front length 36.8, back length 41.9 |
| Best Mannequins COUTF13 dress form with legs (snippet)[^archiexpo-coutf13] | ~36 EU | 91 | 68 | 88 (low hip 92) | neck 33, shoulder width 38, under-bust 79 |
| Greneker form (snippet)[^greneker] | 8-10/M | 91.4 | 73.7 | 99.1 | |
| Display mannequin[^fashionunited] | 4-6 | ~86 | 61-64 | 91 | height 180 |

The Alvanon Intimate/Swim fit form comes in sizes 34-36 with B/C cups, about 15% softer than
standard forms with a widened crotch; no measurements published.[^alvanon]

"Total rise" (front waist through crotch to back waist) is the lower part of a leotard torso loop
