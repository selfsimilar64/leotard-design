# Takeaway

A professional dress form is a **fibreglass or papier-mâché shell**, padded with felt or foam and
covered in **linen or canvas** with **raised or marked style lines**. It has **collapsible or
clip-on shoulder caps (no arms)** and a **cast-iron or aluminium caster base with a foot-pedal
height pole**. Standard bodices end at the hip or upper thigh; **"3/4" or "half-leg" forms, used for
swimwear, add thighs cut at mid-thigh and a real crotch**, which makes them the closest match for a
leotard. **No leotard-specific form was found.**[^fit]

# Construction

| Maker / type | Facts |
|---|---|
| General | Thick fibreglass shell bonded with resin in two side moulds; steel bar from the top to about the waist; needle-wool padding glued on; linen smoothing layer; a "measurement line" added in finishing[^wiki-dressform] |
| PGM | "100% Linen with high density felt (not sponge)", raised side seams, collapsible shoulders, aluminium base, foot-pedal height; arms sold separately[^pgm-missy] |
| The Shop Company | Fibreglass core, felt, canvas exterior, "integrated seam lines", removable clip-in shoulders ("shoulders not arms"), cast-iron base with pedal height[^shopco-child] |
| Stockman | Papier-mâché covered in off-white cotton; antique ones have a turned wooden finial on top[^stdibs] |
| Wolf | Linen pinnable body, metal finishings, adjustable height, cast-iron base[^ualberta] |

**Style lines:** tape round bust, waist, neckline and hip; princess lines from mid-shoulder (front
and back) over the bust point then straight down;[^threads] draping guides also mark centre front,
centre back, armscye, cross shoulder and neckline.[^loosethreads]

# Where the form is cut

- PGM "Industry Grade" has legs; the pole can go through the centre or a leg; measured to max
  thigh, mid thigh, knee.[^pgm-industry] PGM measures abdomen 4 in (10 cm) and hip 8 in (20 cm)
  below the waist.[^pgm-home]
- Shop Co 3/4 forms are a "half-leg body" for rompers and swimsuits.[^shopco-adult]
- Panache "Bloomsbury" 3/4 torso: neck finishes just below the head, legs finish mid-thigh.[^morplan-panache]
- Best Mannequins COUTF13: dress form with legs cut at mid-thigh, removable head and neck block.[^archiexpo-coutf13]

# Display and swim forms

Display forms sit on round metal or wood bases, wood tripods or rolling bases;[^dressformsusa]
child display forms on a wood flat base.[^shopco-display] Swim forms: Barr Display fibreglass
"Brazilian swimsuit torso form" in natural tan;[^barr] Fixtures & Displays fleshtone activewear
counter-top torso, about 30-36 in tall;[^fd-activewear] Alvanon Intimate/Swim fit form, sizes 34-36
with B/C cups, about 15% softer than standard forms with a widened crotch (no measurements
published).[^alvanon]

# Not found, and inferred values

Neck-cap diameter and material, pole diameter and base footprint were not found. **[INFERRED from
general product knowledge]:** neck caps are a turned wood or metal disc about 9-11 cm across,
4-6 cm above the neck base; poles 2.5-3 cm steel tube with a collar; caster bases 3-5 legs spanning
50-60 cm; standard bodice forms end 5-10 cm below the hip line, flat-bottomed with a rim.[^fit]

# Suggested 3D presets [INFERRED]

Cut the body about 5 cm above the neck base with a flat top and a Ø 10 cm, 1.5 cm thick turned-wood
(walnut) cap; cut arms flush at the armscye plane with smooth shoulder caps; use the swim-form cut,
legs cut horizontally at mid-thigh (about half-way crotch → knee, roughly 12-15 cm below the crotch
on an adult, 8-10 cm on the child; "mid-thigh" is sourced, the ratio inferred); natural
linen/oatmeal cover (#D9CDB8, roughness 0.9, weave normal map) with ~6 mm dark style-line tape at
bust, waist, hip, centre front/back, princess lines and side seams; Ø 2.5 cm chrome pole from the
crotch cap, a collar, and a 5-leg caster base about 55 cm across (or a 3-leg wood tripod).[^fit]
