# Takeaway

Vendor charts agree within about 3 cm: **age 8 ≈ 130 cm tall, 64-69 / 56-62 / 68-72 cm
(chest/waist/hip)**; **age 12-13 ≈ 152-155 cm, 76 / 62-66 / 81 cm**; **age 14 ≈ 80 / 71 / 86 cm**.[^fit]
Inch figures were converted to cm by the researchers (1 in = 2.54 cm, rounded). Charts from Bonami,
Window Mannequins, Hans Boodt, Almax, Alvanon children's forms, Wolf and PGM were not found or were
images.[^fit]
