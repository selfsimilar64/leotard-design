# Takeaway

The working garment set is: sleeveless **tank** leotard, **long-sleeve**, **three-quarter-sleeve**
and **short/cap-sleeve** leotards, the **biketard** (short-leg one-piece) and the **unitard**
(ankle-length one-piece), plus optional **shorts or leggings** worn over or under. In US club
competition the three-quarter sleeve is now the dominant competition silhouette; long sleeve is the
elite and NCAA convention; the tank is mainly training wear that is also legal, and sometimes
issued, for competition.[^styles] One catalogue design is routinely sold in tank, three-quarter and
full-sleeve versions, so sleeve length behaves as a parameter, not a different garment.[^sylviap-cat]

# Options

| Garment | Brand terms | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tank (sleeveless) | `Tank Leotards`, `Tank-Style`, `Workout Tank`, `Sleeveless`, `Sleeveless Leotards` | Mainly training; legal at some competitions[^gk-blog-types]; issued at some USA Gymnastics national events (see below) |
| Cap / short sleeve | `cap sleeve`, `Short Sleeve` | Rare in competition lines: GK #2008 offered `cap sleeve or long sleeve`; 12 of 405 Quatro descriptions mention short or cap sleeves[^gk-special][^quatro-all] |
| Three-quarter sleeve | `3Q Sleeve`, `3/4 Sleeve`, `3 Quarter Length Sleeve` | US club competition default |
| Long sleeve | `Full Sleeve`, `Long Sleeve`, `LS`, `Long Sleeve Leos` | Elite and NCAA convention; GK says long sleeves give a polished look and clear lines that show form[^gk-blog-types] |
| Biketard | `biketard` | Leotard with short legs; often a young gymnast's first leotard, chosen for coverage. Snowflake: 15 styles, Girls XS-L[^snowflake-biketards] |
| Unitard | `Unitards` | One-piece with full-length legs, hip to ankle (FIG wording quoted in 2021; Gymnastics NZ the same); USA Gymnastics also recognises a short-leg unitard[^sin-2021][^gnz-attire][^usag-rp] |
| Shorts / leggings | shorts, tights (capri or longer), leggings | Overlay garments worn under or over the leotard; allowed by USA Gymnastics, NCAA and NZ rules[^usag-rp][^ncaa-rules][^gnz-attire] |

Snowflake says competition leotards are usually long-sleeved, more glamorous than workout
leotards, and should fit slightly more snugly.[^snowflake-training] Wikipedia: competition leotards
were traditionally long-sleeved; half-length and sleeveless versions are now permitted under the
Code of Points; practice and podium-training garments are generally sleeveless.[^wiki-leotard] For
Paris 2024 each US long-sleeve competition leotard came with a short-sleeve training
replica.[^nbc-2024] Men's competition wear, for contrast, is two-layer: a sleeveless singlet plus
shorts or stirrup pants.[^wiki-leotard]

# Tanks as competition wear

At USA Gymnastics national events Level 9 Eastern/Western competitors receive a region-coloured
tank to compete in; National Xcelebration participants receive a GK tank and scrunchie; Level 10
Nationals regional team members receive a competitive leotard, scrunchie and warm-up from
GK.[^usag-rp]

# Unitards at elite level

The German women's team debuted ankle-length unitards at the April 2021 European Championships in
Basel and wore them in Tokyo 2020 qualification (25 Jul 2021), framed as a stand against
sexualisation; Sarah Voss is described as the first female gymnast to cover her legs at an
international competition for a non-religious reason.[^sin-2021][^wiki-voss] Destira sells unitards
as a team product.[^destira-rsc]

# Rule limits on garment type

NCAA allows only long-sleeve or sleeveless-tank one-piece leotards; USA Gymnastics names
full-sleeve and sleeveless leotards and unitards. Neither text names the three-quarter sleeve,
although it dominates club catalogues and is sold as compliant. See
[rule conflicts](/domain/rules/rule-conflicts.md).

# Implications for the software [INFERRED]

- Base garment as a small enum: `leotard` / `biketard` / `unitard`, with sleeve length a separate
  parameter and shorts/leggings as overlay garments.[^styles]
- Sleeve length switchable without redesigning body panels.
- A "level preset" could set defaults: NCAA (long or sleeveless only), USAG club (three-quarter
  default), FIG elite (long-sleeve convention, any sleeve legal), training (tank default).[^styles]

# Gaps
