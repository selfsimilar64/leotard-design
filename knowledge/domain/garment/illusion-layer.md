# Takeaway

The "illusion" look is a **mesh layer backed by skin-tone lining** that rises from an **opaque
line** (sweetheart, V or scoop) to a **higher mesh neckline**, so the area reads as skin while the
garment stays covered. The opaque line and the mesh line are two separate design lines.[^styles]
Nude mesh is named as a defining feature of 2020s leotards, and mesh in the upper bodice is now
standard at elite level.[^ncaa-com-2024][^cornell]

# How brands build it

- GK requires a mesh bodice lining in `nude shadowproof` fabric on mesh-bodice styles.[^gk-special]
- Quatro offers three nude tones for leotard lining and mesh.[^quatro-fabrics] 28 of 405 Quatro
  descriptions mention nude lining or mesh.[^quatro-all]
- Sylvia P team listings come in `Deep Nude Lining` and `Light Nude Lining` variants;[^sylviap-us]
  its catalogue builds a `double layer neckline` by mesh overlaid on the bodice, and uses
  `sweetheart V-mesh paneling` and `Sweetheart V-shaped paneling` as overlay lines.[^sylviap-cat]
- Sweetheart appears as an overlay or panel line, not as an open neckline.[^styles]
- Sylvia P lines all mesh body panels in skin lining for zero transparency; sleeves stay unlined.
  See [fabric zones](/domain/garment/fabric-zones-and-panel-lines.md).[^sylviap-cat]

# Where illusion mesh is used beyond the neckline

Upper back, keyhole infill, side panels, waist cut-outs, and as the under-layer for straps and back
twists. See [backs](/domain/garment/backs.md) and [straps](/domain/garment/straps.md).

# Rules

- Gymnastics NZ explicitly permits mesh underlain by skin-coloured fabric.[^gnz-attire]
- USA Gymnastics exempts back openings filled with mesh or flesh-coloured fabric from its
  "backless" test.[^usag-rp]
- **Open:** whether nude-lined mesh above the half-sternum line satisfies the FIG transparency and
  neckline tests is not addressed in any document opened.[^styles] See
  [unverified rules](/domain/rules/unverified-and-missing-rules.md).

# Suggested model [INFERRED]

An `illusion layer` option on the neckline: an opaque line shape and depth, a mesh neckline shape
above it, a mesh fabric, and a selectable nude lining tone. Any torso mesh zone gets a lining layer
automatically.[^styles]
