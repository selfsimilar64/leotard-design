# Takeaway

**A leotard is rarely one person's idea, is drawn flat, and is cut by someone else.** Coaches, athletes and sales staff
often originate and translate a design;[^cgn-2024][^ozone] the only design software named by any source is Adobe
Illustrator and Photoshop;[^nbc][^notes] a line drawing goes to a pattern department that owns fit and cutting.[^nbc]
The evidence is thin and celebratory: no real design sheet is public and no designer criticises the process on record.[^report]

# What the sources show

- **Starting points:** a client's inspiration photo, proofs in team colours, a client's drawing, a catalogue style with a twist; national collections start from an athlete survey and mood boards. Almost nothing starts from a blank body.[^nbc][^ozone][^cgn-2020][^cgn-2024]
- **Order of decisions:** no source supports silhouette, then lines, then fabric, then crystals. Team colours are fixed early, function comes before decoration, fabric swatches follow the mock-up and stones can change up to the last minute.[^report]
- **The back is its own decision:** a new back changes the whole look while the front art and stones stay; OZONE aims for four to five new back patterns a year (one source, sponsored).[^ozone]
- **Hand-off:** one GK designer's job in 2016 included paperwork carrying sewing and embellishment information to pattern makers and the factory floor.[^udaily]
- **Iteration:** client-led, at two stages, drawing and sample. The clearest failure is a sample that matched the request and still changed once seen on a body.[^cgn-2020] OZONE runs live calls where coaches watch stones move and colours change (sponsored).[^ozone]
- **Neighbouring trades:** the best-documented skating route is flat too: the design is drawn on the pattern and pieces are glued flat and stitched. Makers leave the paper when a line's relation to the body matters, by drawing on a test garment on the wearer or taping paper over a bodysuit on a form.[^trades] Theatre researchers who drew style lines on taped shells found proportions read better than in flat cutting, for non-stretch cloth.[^garland]

# What this means for the app **[INFERRED]**

- Offer the real openings: a base style in team colours, a pinned photo, an imported sketch. An empty mannequin matches none of them.
- Drawing on the mannequin is the digital form of makers' improvised test garments, not what leotard designers already do; it must hand back flat pieces, and designers should be expected to ask for flats beside it.
- Each part must change without disturbing the others, because backs and stones change late. A suggested working order may be offered; it must never be enforced.
- The export has to carry intent as well as shapes, because the pattern department reads it.
