# Takeaway

Straps are specified by **count, width, path pattern, what lies beneath them** (bare skin, nude
mesh, or a printed imitation) and **material** (self-fabric binding, rouleau tube, branded elastic,
shiny mystique).[^styles] Thin "spaghetti" straps are banned in USA Gymnastics Development and Xcel
competition (0.20 after a warning),[^usag-rp] and strap-heavy backs can catch a coach's hand while
spotting.[^snowflake-training] Printed or mesh-backed **faux straps** give the strappy look without
either problem **[INFERRED]**.[^styles]

Frequency: straps appear in 112 of 405 Quatro descriptions, crossing straps in 69;[^quatro-all] GK
tanks have about 8 strappy backs of 232.[^gk-tanks]

On tanks the strap or shoulder edge is typically 3-5 cm wide at the shoulder **[INFERRED]**.[^fit]

# Opinion and history

NCAA reviewers (2025): keep one consistent strap width instead of mixing thick and thin; retire
lace-up and threaded straps.[^cgn-2025] Arizona State's 2010s `Pitchfork` leotard with a
spaghetti-strap trident is cited as one of the most polarising NCAA designs.[^ncaa-com-2024]

# Suggested parameters [INFERRED]

`count`; `width` (with a minimum that rules out spaghetti straps); `path` (horizontal ladder,
vertical, single diagonal, X criss-cross, multi-X lattice, V-converging, twisted pair, laced);
`under-layer` (bare, nude mesh, printed imitation); `material`; `anchors` (armhole binding,
neckband/clasp, side seam, lower back edge); `crystal line` on/off.[^styles]

# Gaps
