# Takeaway

As of the 2026 season, NCAA leotards favour **velvet, branded cuffs and collars, asymmetry (single
mesh waist cut-outs, one shoulder) and school-spirit imagery**; reviewers predict **ribbing, split
collars, player names on the back and sleeve stripes** for 2027.[^cgn-2026] Club catalogues for
2026-27 favour **printed ombré bodies with matching sublimated mesh sleeves, asymmetric mesh
paneling, double-layer necklines and backs, belts, fluoro colours and heavy crystal**.[^sylviap-cat]
Trend evidence is editorial (one NCAA outlet) and brand copy, not survey data; revisit by
2027-10.[^styles]

# NCAA

| Season | Trends |
|---|---|
| 2025 (reviewer roundtable) | Ribbed and waffle-textured fabrics, velvet, matte versus sparkle camps, tie-dye, lace-up and criss-cross backs, open and low backs, small cut-outs, coloured single sleeves, ombré sleeves, asymmetry, negative-space and sparkle logos, cursive lettering, architecture-inspired and throwback designs[^cgn-2025] |
| 2026 | Velvet (Clemson velvet bodice with white sleeves; Illinois, Rhode Island College, Utica full-body velvet); team name on cuffs as print (Kent State, Clemson) or rhinestones (UCLA, Missouri); creative school spirit (slogans, hand signs, skylines, landmarks); asymmetry (single mesh waist cut-outs at Illinois, Minnesota, Michigan State, Florida; Auburn's one-shoulder `Bloom`; two-sided monochrome); split collars (Oklahoma, New Hampshire); names on collars[^cgn-2026] |
| 2027 (predicted) | Ribbing; more collars, split collars; personalisation (player names on the back); sleeve stripes (Towson racing stripe, Utah State rhinestone stripe, Oklahoma tapered white panel over mesh sleeves)[^cgn-2026] |

Names and slogans are legal under NCAA rules but not FIG; see
[rule conflicts](/domain/rules/rule-conflicts.md).

# Club level (2026-27)

- Sylvia P catalogue: two- and three-colour printed ombré bodies with matching sublimated mesh
  sleeves; asymmetric mesh paneling; double-layer necklines and backs; belts; neon/fluoro colours;
  heavy-crystal versions of about 800 to 2,900 stones per leotard.[^sylviap-cat][^sylviap-fabrics]
- Embellishment range in 2026: round crystals SS6-SS34; shaped crystals (navette, square, kite, pear
  at Sylvia P; boat and waterdrop at Quatro); sequins 2-5 mm in iridescent, holographic, glitter and
  mirror finishes; GK `Spanglez`; nailheads; embroidery; pearls.[^sylviap-emb][^quatro-stones][^gk-9661s]
  Details in [/domain/embellishments/](/domain/embellishments/about.md).
- Athlete-designed and signature lines: GK (Simone Biles, Jade Carey, Hezly Rivera); Quatro (Helen
  Kevric's `Mariposa`, Lorette Charpy's `Aquarius`); Snowflake (college gymnasts).[^gk-home][^quatro-all][^snowflake-training]
