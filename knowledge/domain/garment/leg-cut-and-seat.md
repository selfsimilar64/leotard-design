# Takeaway

Brands offer two named leg cuts per style (GK: **traditional/workout** vs **modified**; Sylvia P:
**standard** vs **mid cut**), but **none defines them geometrically**.[^styles] Rules cap how high
the opening can rise: USA Gymnastics requires the leg cut **below the pelvis/hip bone**.[^usag-rp]
Keeping the seat from riding up is treated as a core engineering problem.[^cornell][^gk-history]

Whether GK's `modified` is higher or lower than `traditional`, and how `Mid Cut` differs from
`Standard`, could not be confirmed.[^styles]

# Rule ceiling

- USA Gymnastics 2026-27: leg cut below the pelvis/hip bone.[^usag-rp] GK's 2026 dress-code guide:
  the leg opening cannot be cut or rolled above the hip.[^gk-dresscode]
- Wikipedia's summary of the Code: conforming leotards cannot be cut above hip height.[^wiki-leotard]
- FIG wording for 2025-2028 was not retrieved; see [FIG rules](/domain/rules/fig-attire-rules.md).
  The FIG aerobic code (outside seam through the iliac crest) is the closest verified analogue.[^fit]

# Fit and comfort

- Diaz: fabrics are tested so that no leg lines ride up and no seams are weak.[^cornell] GK's
  company history cites keeping leotard seats down as a signature engineering problem.[^gk-history]
- Crystal placement avoids the leg line and underarms for comfort (2024 US Olympic leotards; from a
  search summary).[^aol-2024]
- Typical geometry (side peak at or just below the iliac crest; front crosses the inguinal crease;
  "full back" covers about two-thirds of the buttock) is **[INFERRED]**; see
  [how a leotard sits on the body](/domain/sizing/how-a-leotard-sits-on-the-body.md).[^fit]

# Coverage alternatives

Shorts or leggings may be worn with the leotard in NCAA (Rule 1.10.d), USA Gymnastics and New
Zealand domestic competition; the unitard removes the leg line entirely.[^ncaa-rules][^usag-rp][^gnz-attire]

# Suggested model [INFERRED]

Leg opening as a curve with a `side height` parameter measured against the hip bone, a hard ceiling
for USA Gymnastics compliance, and named presets (traditional/workout, modified, standard, mid). A
no-crystal mask along the leg opening and underarm by default.[^styles]
