# Takeaway

> Deferred: rule checking is out of scope since 2026-10-13
> ([decision](/decisions/2026-10-13-defer-competition-rules-and-pain-points.md)).

NCAA allows only **matching one-piece leotards, long sleeve or sleeveless tank**; it explicitly
**allows names and numbers** in a team-wide font and **shorts or leggings** in any combination. An
uncorrected infraction costs **0.2** from the team (or the individual).[^ncaa-rules]

| Rule | Content (paraphrased) |
|---|---|
| 1.10.a | Official uniform and all apparel identical on a practice or competition day; any combination allowed if it conforms and matches teammates; identical warm-ups for march-in and awards; in postseason individual competition, same-team athletes wear identical uniforms and leotards[^ncaa-rules] |
| 1.10.b | Name and/or number may appear on warm-ups and leotard if the whole team uses a similar font type and colour; no nicknames or other words; last name only, or first initial plus last name (exceptions for duplicates)[^ncaa-rules] |
| 1.10.c | Matching one-piece leotards, **long sleeve or sleeveless tank**; undergarments in the leotard's colour or skin tone; no bathing suits[^ncaa-rules] |
| 1.10.d | Spandex shorts or long leggings/tights may be worn in any combination with the leotard if they conform to the official uniform and match when more than one athlete wears them[^ncaa-rules] |
| 1.10.e | Meet Referee informs the head coach; if not corrected, **0.2** team neutral deduction (team competition) or 0.2 neutral deduction on the individual's score[^ncaa-rules] |
| 1.11 | Stud piercings only; 0.2 per occurrence after a warning[^ncaa-rules] |
| 1.2 | Otherwise USA Gymnastics Level 10 rules as of 1 Nov 2026 apply; NCAA modifications take precedence[^ncaa-rules] |

The explicit shorts/tights/leggings allowance was reported as a clarification for the 2025
season (search snippet).[^cgn-2025-clar]

**Not stated:** how three-quarter-sleeve or one-shoulder designs are treated. Both appear in NCAA
practice (Auburn's one-shoulder `Bloom`, 2026), so enforcement may be looser than the text, but no
source says so.[^styles] Because Rule 1.2 imports USA Gymnastics Level 10 rules, whether the USA
Gymnastics backless/racerback provisions reach NCAA is unclear **[INFERRED]**.
