# Takeaway

> Deferred: rule checking is out of scope since 2026-10-13
> ([decision](/decisions/2026-10-13-defer-competition-rules-and-pain-points.md)).

National bodies add their own domestic rules; FIG rules apply instead at FIG events. New Zealand is
the most permissive documented case (individual choice of leotard, unitard or added shorts within a
team). Across all rule sets a designer can offer modesty options: unitard, leg coverings, shorts,
tight head covering, and mesh-filled or nude-lined openings.[^styles]

# New Zealand (domestic; reviewed after 2023 feedback)

- Leotard or hip-to-ankle unitard in club or representative colours; sleeveless, short or long
  sleeves; mesh underlain by skin-coloured fabric permitted.[^gnz-attire]
- Shorts or leggings may be worn over the leotard (black or the leotard's primary colour, plain or
  with a discreet logo); no deduction for visible underwear.[^gnz-attire]
- Team designs identical, but individuals may choose leotard, unitard or added shorts/leggings;
  own-choice leotard on day two of two-day meets; hip or other padding not permitted.[^gnz-attire]
- Penalties: **0.3** by the control judge per exercise after the athlete has a chance to fix it;
  **1.00** for non-identical team leotards, once per competition phase.[^gnz-attire][^gnz-guide]

# Germany

The national team wore ankle-length unitards from April 2021 as a deliberate statement against
sexualisation.[^sin-2021] See [garment types](/domain/garment/garment-types-and-silhouettes.md).

# Modesty options by rule set

| Option | FIG | USA Gymnastics | NCAA | NZ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unitard | yes | yes (ankle or short legs) | – (one-piece leotards named) | yes |
| Full leg coverings in leotard colour | yes | tights capri or longer | long leggings/tights | leggings |
| Shorts over/under | on shorts, advertising rules apply | yes | spandex shorts | yes, over |
| Tight head covering in leotard colours | yes (Appendix E) | – | – | – |
| Mesh-filled / nude-lined openings | not addressed | exempts back openings from "backless" | – | yes |

Sources: FIG clothing rules, USA Gymnastics Rules & Policies, NCAA Rule 1.10, Gymnastics NZ.[^fig-clothing][^usag-rp][^ncaa-rules][^gnz-attire]
