# Takeaway

A leotard is a closed loop over the shoulder and through the crotch, so its fit is controlled by
the **torso loop**: from mid-shoulder down the front, through the crotch, up the back to the same
shoulder. GK and Quatro both call it **the most important measurement**.[^gk-sizepage][^quatro-size]
Torso length decides whether a leotard "rides up or digs in".[^gmd]

- **Definition:** loop from mid-shoulder down the front, through the crotch, up the back to the
  same shoulder.[^gk-sizechart][^fit]
- **Range:** about 37-39 in (94-99 cm) at GK `CXXS` to 69-71 in (175-180 cm) at `A4XL`; see the
  [GK chart](/domain/sizing/gk-and-nike-size-chart.md).
- **Measuring rules:** round partial inches up; if between sizes choose the larger; measuring the
  whole team gives a similar fit for all (GK); Quatro also rounds up.[^gk-sizepage][^quatro-size]
  Sylvia P says do not add growing room.[^styles]
- **When torso disagrees with girths,** brands offer long- and short-torso variants rather than a
  different size; see [torso variants](/domain/sizing/torso-variants-and-sizing-sets.md).

**On a 3D body [INFERRED]:** measure the mesh's mid-shoulder → crotch → mid-shoulder geodesic loop
to assign a size (e.g. the POC child body ≈ 120 cm → `CM`).[^fit] See
[body targets by age](/domain/sizing/body-measurement-targets-by-age.md).
