# Takeaway

GK Elite's chart runs **13 sizes from CXXS to A4XL**, by chest, waist, hip and **torso** (the most
important measurement).[^gk-chart-pdf] Nike's gymnastics torso ranges are identical to GK's
(GK runs a Nike collaboration line, which likely explains it **[INFERRED]**), and Nike adds
heights.[^fit][^nike][^styles] The chart is headed `SLEEK FIT`
and dated March 2015.
