# Takeaway

Made-to-order teamwear comes in each size with **regular, long-torso and short-torso** variants
(GK: about 3 inches longer or shorter).[^gk-chart-pdf] **Fit differs by fabric family**: GK
specifies one sizing set for plain nylon/spandex, velvet or `PolyTek` and a different one for
`Mystique`, `Hologram` or `FoilTek`, because foil-coated fabrics stretch differently.[^gk-sizing-set]
Clubs size whole teams with physical sizing kits before ordering.[^styles]

# Torso variants

- GK: each size has a long-torso option (torso about 3 in longer, e.g. `CM` 49-51 in) and a
  short-torso option (about 3 in shorter, e.g. `CM` 43-45 in); **not available on in-stock
  garments**.[^gk-chart-pdf]
- Sylvia P offers long and short torso for women's teamwear.[^sylviap-sizing]

# Measuring rules

| Brand | Measure | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| GK | chest, waist, hip, torso (most important) | Round partial inches up; between sizes choose the larger; measure the whole team for similar fit[^gk-sizepage][^gk-chart-pdf] |
| Sylvia P | chest, waist **at the navel**, hip, torso | Do not add growing room (it distorts sizing); between sizes choose the larger[^sylviap-sizing] |
| Quatro | torso most important | Round partial measurements up (see [other brands](/domain/sizing/other-brand-size-systems.md)) |

# Sizing sets, fit kits, sizers

- GK sizing set reference guide (2015): separate women's competitive sizing sets for plain
  (nylon/spandex, velvet, `PolyTek`) versus foil (`Mystique`, `Hologram`, `FoilTek`) primary
  fabrics. GK also promotes Fit Kits for team orders.[^gk-sizing-set][^gk-home]
- Sylvia P: clubs can request a sizing kit; fabric, product and fit samples offered.[^sylviap-sizing][^sylviap-diff]
- Snowflake lends sizers to teams against a credit card so parents know what to order.[^snowflake-team]
- Competition leotards are often bought a size smaller than practice ones (forum, search summary).[^chalkbucket-sizing]

# Fit standard and per-order options

- GK: competition apparel needs a **second-skin fit** (warm-ups do not); a leotard should be secure
  and close without restricting movement or needing adjustment.[^gk-sizing-set][^gk-blog-types]
- Sylvia P per-order fit options: leg cut (`Standard` / `Mid Cut`) and an optional built-in **shelf
  bra** (213 of 1,170 US listings are shelf-bra variants; AUD 12.60 extra on the Australian
  site).[^sylviap-sibella][^sylviap-us]
- Lettering scales by character count, not garment size, in Destira's budget programme (8-9 letters
  per row for gym names, 3-4 for initials).[^destira-rsc]

# Implications [INFERRED]

- About 13 graded sizes, each with regular/long/short torso, driven by four measurements with torso
  dominant.[^styles]
- The pattern (or its stretch allowance) should be a function of the primary fabric's stretch, not
  only size; see [stretch and ease](/domain/sizing/stretch-and-negative-ease.md) and
  [/domain/fabrics/](/domain/fabrics/about.md).
