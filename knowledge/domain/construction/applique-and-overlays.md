# Takeaway

**Appliqué on leotards is a stretch-on-stretch overlay held temporarily with adhesive and then
stitched, done on flat pieces before assembly.** Factories laser-cut the shapes; ateliers cut by hand.
Reverse, padded and mesh-backed variants exist. Heat-transfer vinyl and foil are low-temperature
add-ons with limited stretch tolerance. No evidence was found of permanently bonded (film-laminated)
construction in WAG leotards.[^pc-notes]

# Techniques

| Technique | How it is made |
|---|---|
| Factory appliqué (GK) | Laser-cut shapes placed by hand with glue, position checked, then stitched; one Tokyo 2021 leotard carried 76 appliqué stars[^nbc-2021] |
| Atelier zigzag appliqué | Cut shape with a spray-stuck paper pattern; tack to the piece with repositionable spray adhesive (Odif 505; washable glue stick on mesh); zigzag very close to the edge, medium width and length, thread matching the appliqué, stretching both layers together; multi-colour designs built one layer at a time, trimming the layer underneath to reduce bulk[^porter-applique] |
| Reverse appliqué | Cut the shape out of the main fabric, back the hole with contrast about 1 in larger all round, zigzag round the opening in thread matching the main fabric; used when contrast would show through or the base is heavily textured[^porter-applique] |
| Padded appliqué | Thin two-way-stretch foam (typically ⅛ in neoprene) bonded under the appliqué fabric, edge wrapped and zigzagged; applied after colour blocking and flat appliqué, before full assembly. Appliqué fabric and padding must have greatest stretch in the same direction or the patch becomes non-stretch; larger appliqués need more stretch[^porter-padded] |
| Mesh-backed cut-out (illusion) | 1 in overlap, glue, appliqué stitching[^porter-neck] |
| Overlay band | Band laid on the front piece and zigzagged along both long edges before assembly[^babylock] |
| Home alternatives | Spray glue (gums the needle) or wash-away stabiliser, then zigzag[^chalkbucket] |
| Heat-transfer vinyl | Siser EasyWeed Stretch (85 µm, shown as discontinued in North America in Oct 2026): 320 °F / 160 °C, firm pressure, 20 s, rated for Lycra/spandex[^siser]. A 2026 guide: stretch films on spandex at 270–305 °F for 10–15 s, light–medium pressure, cold peel; max two layers in high-stretch areas; standard, glitter and flocked films not stretch-compatible; keep transfers small and in low-stretch areas[^printable-press] |
| Hand foil embossing | Flexible adhesive (Fabri-Fuse or clear water-based caulk) piped onto the stretched, finished garment, cured to tacky, foil rubbed on; hand-wash only[^porter-foil] |
| Embroidery | GK embroiders logos after piece decoration and before crystals; one-time set-up fee[^nam-2024][^gk-faq] |

All via the research note.[^pc-notes] Long narrow appliqués may need their stretch running lengthwise,
an exception to the around-the-body rule.[^porter-padded][^pc-notes]

# Appliqué export **[INFERRED]**

- Appliqué cut contour (no seam allowance) as its own piece, with grain/stretch direction.
- Placement outline on the base piece, layer order, and edge stitch type.
- Stitchability limit: minimum feature width of roughly two zigzag widths, no slivers; no published
  minimum exists, so this is a manufacturer setting.
- Treat appliqué area as reduced-stretch in any fit estimate and require its stretch direction to
  match the base.

# Gaps
