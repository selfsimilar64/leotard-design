# Takeaway

**The footage confirms the factory works on a point-and-curve CAD pattern and cuts from nested
markers, and hints that one block carries several style lines; it shows nothing of how the block
was drafted.** All videos the owner found are ten or more years old.[^owner-2026-11-05] The frames
are blurry and partly interpreted, so the readings below are **[INFERRED]** unless stated.

# GK Elite, "Precision Pattern Making" (about 2010)

`gk-cad-back-piece.png` (and the titled frame `gk-precision-pattern-making-title.png`):[^owner-frames]

- A piece labelled **BK** (back) on a CAD screen, drawn as straight segments between corner points
  (squares) and curves through intermediate points (crosses), the same representation as our
  drafted blocks.
- The piece lies with its length across the screen; a green grain or stretch arrow runs along it.
- Inside the outline: several near-parallel straight lines across the piece and **two curves leaving
  the same top corner**, one shallow and one a deep U. This reads as one back block carrying
  alternative openings or panel lines, not graded nests (a grade nest would offset the whole outline).
  If so, it supports the [block plus moves](/domain/construction/real-leotard-yardsticks.md) model:
  styles drawn as lines on a shared block.

`gk-marker-on-screen.png`: a long marker of outlined pieces in several colours (probably sizes or
styles), mirrored pairs side by side, long thin strips (bindings or straps). Too blurry to identify
leotard pieces; it may include warm-up garments. Consistent with
[cutting and marker making](/domain/construction/cutting-and-marker-making.md).

# Alpha Factor, FloGymnastics "How It's Made" (2016)

`alpha-factor-marker-flogymnastics.png`:[^owner-frames][^flo-alpha-factor]

- A marker of filled pieces, one colour per size or style, nested head to toe.
- Several body pieces show **both leg openings and the crotch between them**, so they appear to be
  cut full width (both halves), not as half pieces on a fold. Our pattern export should be able to
  give mirrored full pieces.
- Unplaced to the right: a large rounded tapering piece (probably a long sleeve), two long
  rectangles (bindings or strips) and two small curved pieces (unidentified; possibly crotch lining
  or panels).

# Use

Low as a yardstick for measurements (no scale), moderate as evidence of how factory patterns are
represented. Clearer frames of the same CAD screens, or of cut pieces on a table, would be worth
more than new videos of sewing. See [yardsticks from real leotards](/domain/construction/real-leotard-yardsticks.md).
