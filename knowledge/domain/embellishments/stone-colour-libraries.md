# Takeaway

Full manufacturer charts are large (Swarovski 2088: about 127 entries in 2018; Preciosa MAXIMA
"100+ colors and coatings" in 2026), but leotard makers offer short menus — Quatro 21 colours,[^quatro-stones]
GK 18 premium + 6 vibrant Swarovski colours plus standard crystals[^gk-blog-emb][^report] — and a
single Olympic leotard used six colours.[^nbcdfw] A practical library needs a few dozen entries,
each decomposed into body colour + backing + coating (see [coatings](/domain/embellishments/crystal-coatings-and-ab.md)).
Current (2026/2027) full charts were **not** retrieved; the Swarovski list predates its restructuring.[^ecs]

# Preciosa

- MAXIMA: "more than 100 colors and coatings"; size-specific glass recipes keep colour consistent
  from 3ss to 48ss.[^ru-colour]
- One EU retailer's MAXIMA hotfix subset (23; categories Colors, Colors AB, Colors Coated, Crystal,
  Crystal Coated): Blue Zircon, Capri Blue, Caribbean Sea, Chrysolite, Cobalt Blue, Crystal, Crystal
  AB, Crystal Argent Flare, Crystal Aurum, Crystal Blond Flare, Crystal Honey, Crystal NightFall,
  Emerald, Gold Quartz, Indian Pink, Jet, Jonquil, Light Colorado Topaz, Light Siam, Light Smoked
  Topaz, Montana, Siam, Smoked Topaz.[^ibeads]
- Preciosa e-shop VIVA hotfix (17): Crystal, Jet, Light Colorado Topaz, Light Sapphire, Crystal AB,
  Peridot, Aquamarine, Black Diamond, Rose, Jonquil, Light Peach, Vintage Rose, Violet, Light
  Amethyst, Light Siam, Topaz, Gold Quartz.[^preciosa-eshop]
- New in 2026: "Chocolate", deepest of a skin-tone palette (Light Gold Quartz → Smoked Topaz →
  Chocolate), MAXIMA hotfix and no-hotfix ss5–ss34; a "2027 MAXIMA … Color Sample Card" is on sale.[^ru-chocolate][^ru-last]

# Suggested default WAG palette

**[INFERRED]**:[^ecs] Crystal, Crystal AB, Jet, Jet Hematite; gold/skin neutrals (Light Colorado
Topaz, Gold Quartz, Smoked Topaz, Chocolate); reds Siam/Light Siam; blues Sapphire/Capri Blue/
Cobalt/Montana; Rose/Fuchsia; Emerald/Peridot; Amethyst/Tanzanite; plus AB versions of the main
colours. Colour-matching stone to fabric intensifies the fabric colour; contrast makes the design
stand out (see [placement vocabulary](/domain/embellishments/placement-vocabulary.md)).

GK's own crystal colour list ("brilliance" vs "clear") was not found.[^ecs]
