# Takeaway

Stone size ("ss", stone size) is a shared standard with small brand tolerances. The apparel working
range is **ss6 (2.0 mm) to ss30 (6.4 mm)**; **ss16 (3.9 mm) and ss20 (4.7 mm) are the workhorses**;
ss34–ss48 are focal stones.[^ecs] SS is the apparel convention, PP (pearl plate) the jewellery one.[^wiki-rhinestone]

The proof of concept uses ss6 2.0, ss10 2.8, ss12 3.1, ss16 3.9, ss20 4.7, ss30 6.4 mm (constant

# Which sizes are used

- Leotards: ss16, ss20 and ss30, smaller stones scattered as gap fillers.[^cp-stretch]
- 20ss and 30ss named most popular; recommended design set 12, 16, 20, 30, 40ss; 40ss "when you
  want maximum impact for stage or costuming".[^ru-whatsize]
- Customer preference 4 mm (ss16), then 3 mm (ss10), then 7 mm (ss34); ss16 gives "fairly good
  filled lettering"; ss10 hard to handle by hand.[^edutech-hotfix]
- Production setter ships with hoppers for SS6 ×2, SS10 ×2, SS16, SS20 and handles "2mm - 5mm".[^coldesi-cams]
- **[UNVERIFIED]** Stock transfer designs are built on SS10 and resized by swapping stone size.[^trw-learn]
- Brand menus (from the report): Quatro SS6–SS20, round/boat/waterdrop, 21 colours;[^quatro-stones]
  Sylvia P SS6, SS10, SS16, SS20, SS34, clear or AB, plus navette, square, kite, pear.[^sylviap-emb][^report]
- **[INFERRED]** Machine-set fills are probably mostly ss6–ss20; ss30+ and shaped stones are
  hand-placed accents, consistent with GK placing "larger crystals by hand".[^ecs]

No WAG maker publishes which ss sizes it uses; the use statements come from crystal retailers.[^ecs]
