# Takeaway

**Fabric is a small part of a leotard's price: an adult sleeved leotard uses about 1.3-1.6 m of
150 cm cloth, roughly $15-37 at US retail, against $100-500 for the finished garment; crystals,
labour and design dominate.**[^notes-fab] **[INFERRED]** (the researchers' arithmetic). All prices
were read on 13 October 2026.

# Finished-garment prices (context)

GK special-order competition leotard $268;[^gk-7659] Snowflake value competition $99.95, competition
$174.95-354.95;[^snowflake-team] Destira custom $100-250;[^destira] some 2012 US Olympic leotards "in the
neighborhood of $500".[^buzzfeed-2012] **[INFERRED]** Fabric is perhaps 5-15% of a $250-500 competition
leotard; a cost estimator can use per-family price bands and an area-plus-waste model, with an upcharge
flag for mystique over plain tricot (matching Plum's policy).[^notes-fab]

**Gaps:** no maker publishes consumption or marker efficiency; mill-direct prices and per-fabric
upcharges at GK, Sylvia P and Quatro are not public.[^notes-fab]
