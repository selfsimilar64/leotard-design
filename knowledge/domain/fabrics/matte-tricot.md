# Takeaway

**Matte tricot is the workhorse: main bodies, contrast panels, binding, piping and training
leotards, and the fabric makers rate as most resilient.** It is a smooth, matte, saturated dyed
warp knit of 78-80% polyamide (nylon) / 20-22% elastane at 190-220 g/m², opaque, with four-way
stretch that is strongly unequal by direction (mill data 85-110% across the width, 145-195% along the
length at 3.6 kg).[^notes-fab] It "pairs well with mystique lycra as a contrast of the non shiny to
the high shine of foil" (Snowflake).[^snowflake-matte]

# Trends

GK's design director reports athletes now prefer matte fabrics under crystals and that shiny fabrics
are becoming less of a default.[^vogue-scandi][^report] **[UNVERIFIED]** (single interview, read via
the report only). Separately, GK's print catalogue shows only 2 Primatex colours against 22 in its
swatch store; whether Primatex is being run down in favour of SoftLux could not be
confirmed.[^notes-fab] See [GK catalogue](/domain/fabrics/gk-elite-fabric-catalogue.md).

**[INFERRED]** Sylvia P's matt lycra may be Carvico Vita or Malaga: one colour name ("Sporting Red")
coincides with a Carvico shade; only that one overlap was verified.[^notes-fab]
