# Takeaway

**Shiny tricot is the same knit as matte tricot with a brighter dye finish, stepping up through
satin-effect to coated "wet look" and latex-like "liquid".** Composition is 76-83% nylon / 17-24%
elastane at 180-220 g/m², opaque, used for bodies, accents and binding.[^notes-fab] Shine without foil
is the current direction at the largest US maker: GK's items marked NEW in 2026-27 are Cire, Stretch
Satin and two SoftLux colours.[^gk-p126][^gk-p128][^notes-fab] **[INFERRED]** (the researchers' reading
of the catalogue).

# Gloss ladder (supplier language, lowest to highest)

1. "Non Shiny nylon lycra" and ultra-matt print bases (see [matte tricot](/domain/fabrics/matte-tricot.md)).
2. Subtle glow rather than high shine: GK Stretch Satin (NEW 2026-27), 76% nylon / 24% spandex,
   binding only.[^gk-swatch][^gk-p128]
3. "satin-effect fabric with extraordinary brightness": Carvico Beverly.[^carvico-beverly]
4. "High-shine" with a coated look: GK Cire (NEW 2026-27), 83% nylon / 17% spandex, tagline
   "High-shine. High-performance. All confidence.", binding and piping.[^gk-swatch][^gk-p126]
5. "a latex-like appearance with a glossy coating over stretch fabric": The Lineup "Liquid", whose
   "subtle stretch properties allow you to move, but they are limited".[^lineup-novelty]
6. Continuous metallic foil (see [solid metallic foil](/domain/fabrics/solid-metallic-foil.md)).

All also in the notes.[^notes-fab]

**[INFERRED]** Beverly needs 145-270 g to reach 40% width extension against 95-180 g for Sumatra, so
the satin-effect tricot is noticeably firmer than plain shiny tricot of similar weight.[^notes-fab]

**[UNVERIFIED]** The shading notes flag that the trade meaning of "wet look" (high-gloss, often coated
nylon/spandex) was assumed from general knowledge rather than a mill source; the fabric notes support
it only through maker copy (Sylvia P, GK Cire "coated look").[^notes-fab]
