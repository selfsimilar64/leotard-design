# Takeaway

**Brand ranges are small and enumerable: 30-46 mystique colours, 11-36 matte, 8-13 wet look, 6-23
mesh (with 3-5 skin tones) and 44-72 sublimation colours per maker; these are good defaults for the
app's library sizes.**[^notes-fab] **[INFERRED]** (the researchers' summary across makers). Colour
names are maker-specific (see [base cloth + finish](/domain/fabrics/base-cloth-and-finish-model.md)).
GK is in its own concept: [GK Elite catalogue](/domain/fabrics/gk-elite-fabric-catalogue.md).

# Snowflake Designs (USA)

Swatch shop of 98 items at $0.25 each in six families: Mystique 30, Shiny Lycra 19, Matte "Sportivo"
Lycra 22 listings ("Many more colors to choose from than we show here."), Starlet Mystique 9 (Grapeade,
Blacklight, Fiery Orchid, Flashbulb, Fuchsia, Heatlamp Red, Limelight, Midnight Magic, Turquoise),
Sparkle Hologram 8, Cracked Ice Hologram 9.[^snowflake-swatches] Programme: stock, customised-stock or
custom with in-house sublimation; "We do NOT charge for customizing colors."; custom 6-8 weeks; rush
+20%; team discount 10% (12-23 pieces), 15% (24-35), 20% (36-47).[^snowflake-team] Full Snowflake colour
lists are in the research notes.[^notes-fab]
