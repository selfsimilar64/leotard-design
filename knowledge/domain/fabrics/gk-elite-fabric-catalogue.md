# Takeaway

**GK Elite (USA; Elite Sportswear, L.P.) publishes the most complete fabric catalogue: a 144-page
2026-27 Competitive and Stock Catalog with fabric pages 126-129 and a sublimation guide from p.130,
backed by an online swatch store with wider colour lists.**[^gk-catalog][^notes-fab] It gives
compositions, colour codes and decoration restrictions, never weights or stretch. Colours were read
by eye from page images; spellings are as printed.[^notes-fab] Page images follow the pattern
`https://secure.viewer.zmags.com/services/resource/pub/167be069/pg2358x2896/3/{page}?viewerID=448752a2`.

# Swatch store (wider than print)

Mystique 41 (adds Magenta Pearl, Gunmetal); Primatex 22 (Burgundy, Red, Fluorescent Pink, Berry,
Fluorescent Coral, Fluorescent Orange, Fluorescent Yellow, Fluorescent Lime, Seaglass, Aqua,
Turquoise, Periwinkle, Royal, Navy, Bright Fuschia, Lavender, Magenta, Purple, White, Light Gray, Gray,
Black); Hologram 15 (adds Paprika Opalescence, Jet Opalescence); SoftLux 18; Mesh 12; Mesh Foil 4;
sublimation colour "blankets" Subfuse, Optic White, Holotek, Opalescence, White Rib, Mesh, Mesh Foil,
Fleece Color. Consumers may take 5 free swatches, dealers, schools and clubs 20; extras $0.25
each.[^gk-swatch] **[INFERRED]** Either the store is the superset of orderable colours, or Primatex is
being run down in favour of SoftLux; unconfirmed.[^notes-fab]

# Design routes and lead times

UDesign Studio routes: Special Order ("Choose your fabric, color, embellishments and details"),
Infinite Possibilities (pick front and back, then fabrics, colours, embellishments), and Custom Designs
("For a one-time, flat rate fee, we'll design your garment, make multiple revisions, and send a fully
embellished sample").[^gk-p141] Production: samples 3 weeks, stock with embellishments 3-4, made to
order 5-6, custom 7-8 weeks.[^gk-production] Special-order fabric-set example:
[fabric stack](/domain/fabrics/fabric-stack.md).

**Not captured:** pages 116, 118-119, 121-125, 134-136 were only skimmed (embellishment and print
pages); the WebGL UDesign fabric picker was not exercised, so per-style fabric-zone restrictions are
unknown.[^notes-fab]
