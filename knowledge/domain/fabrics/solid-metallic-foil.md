# Takeaway

**Solid foil is the all-over version of the foil finish: a continuous metallic layer (or glossy
coating) on an 80/20 nylon-spandex base, used for accents.** About 184 g/m², four-way stretch "but
limited", continuous metallic or latex-like gloss, opaque.[^notes-fab] Garland notes that metallic
garments may fit differently.[^garland]

|---|---|---|---|
| Spandex World 4 Way Metallic foil-shiny (Silver) | 80/20 nylon spandex | listed as 6.5 oz/yd², 184 g/m² and 340 g/yd (inconsistent; treat GSM as approximate) | "Silver shiny metallic foil all over on white nylon spandex."; $15.00/yd[^sw-1214] |
| The Lineup "Anaconda" | black 4-way nylon spandex base | n/p | "metallic foil over a black 4 way stretch nylon spandex, this fabric catches the light beautifully" (Black Silver, Black Gold)[^lineup-novelty] |
