# Takeaway

**Foil and glitter meshes put sparse glints on a sheer ground; they inherit mesh's fragility and the
foil's sensitivity.** Typical make-up 80% PES / 20% EA (GK) or net with foil dots, about 115 g/m²,
four-way, sheer with sparkle; used for sleeves and overlays.[^notes-fab]

| Product | Composition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GK Mesh Foil ("Pixie and Fairy mesh") | 80% PES / 20% spandex | "Spanglez cannot be applied to mesh foil"; 4 colours: 01G Pine Fairy, 10D Merlot Fairy, 87E White Opal, 88E Black Opal; no binding or piping[^gk-p127][^gk-swatch] |
| GK Novelty Mesh (Jacquard, Smocked) | 58% nylon / 42% spandex (Smocked: 95% nylon / 5% spandex) | "Spanglez cannot be applied to novelty mesh"; White and Black Jacquard, Black Smocked[^gk-p127] |
| GK Techmesh | 80% nylon / 20% spandex | black; available in binding and piping[^gk-p127] |
| Funki PF1022 Glint Foiled Net | 83/17 PES, 115 gsm | dotted metallic silver foil that can shift colour with certain inks[^funki-bases] |
| The Lineup Metallic Mesh / Glitter Mesh | n/p | "a 4-way stretch mesh fabric with a shiny foil-like sheen"; glitter "will shed and may dull over time and with use"; heat-pressing rhinestones onto metallic mesh dulls its shine[^lineup-mesh] |
| DSI London Metallic Mesh | n/p | woven-in metallic finish said not to rub or wash off[^dsi-mesh] |
| Sylvia P Fog Mesh / Diamond Mesh | n/p | Fog: metallic foil finish, 5 colours, delicate; Diamond: patterned 4-way, sensitive, White and Black[^sp-fabric] |

A GK training style labels its jacquard mesh overlay "54% nylon/42% spandex", conflicting with the
catalogue's 58/42.[^gk-3885][^gk-p127] All rows also in the notes.[^notes-fab]

Observed in swatch photos (researcher's own reading): fog and fairy mesh show sparse glints on a
sheer ground.[^notes-fab]
