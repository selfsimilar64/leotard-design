# Takeaway

**Glitter tricot is the cheapest route to all-over sparkle, and the least durable: glitter sheds and
dulls.** 80% nylon / 20% elastane, 216 g/m², four-way, opaque, used for accents and
training.[^notes-fab]

- Spandex World Glitter Milliskin: "Silver glitter all over on black nylon spandex.", 80/20,
  6.37 oz/yd² (216 GSM), 4-way, $15-16/yd; the listing warns the shiny finish may fade after wear and
  washing.[^sw-21628]
- Glitter on mesh "will shed and may dull over time and with use" (The Lineup, glitter
  mesh).[^lineup-mesh] See [foil and glitter mesh](/domain/fabrics/foil-and-glitter-mesh.md).
- Related sequin-type yardage: Spandex World Holographic Dots, "Multicolored holographic sequins on
  black nylon spandex.", 80/20, listed as 6 oz/yd² and 170 GSM, 4-way, $15.00/yd.[^sw-21733]

All also in the notes.[^notes-fab] **[INFERRED]** On durability, glitter sits with mesh, foil mesh and
lace as the most fragile group.[^notes-fab] No flake size, density or shape data was found for any
glitter fabric.[^notes-fab]

procedural glitter is described in [fabric shader](/engineering/poc/fabric-shader.md).
