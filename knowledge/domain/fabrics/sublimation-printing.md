# Takeaway

**Sublimation only works on white (or light) polyester, which is why every maker's range splits into
a nylon foil family and a polyester print family.** The design is printed on wide-format transfer
paper and transferred into the cloth with a heat rotary calender; Funki states the process is for
white polyester-based fabrics only and calls it a dry process.[^funki-bases][^notes-fab] Printed
leotards therefore use [polyester print bases](/domain/fabrics/polyester-print-base.md), and printable
foil and hologram fabrics are polyester versions of the nylon originals.[^notes-fab]

# How it works

- Common sublimation dyes activate at 350 F / 175 C; 380-420 F / 195-215 C is normally recommended
  for optimal colour; works on polyester and polymer-coated substrates; the print "will not crack,
  fade or peel from the substrate under normal conditions."[^wiki-sub]
- Why not nylon: sublimation is done on polyester "because it can withstand higher temperatures",
  while nylon is wet-printed or digitally direct-printed, e.g. with acid inks.[^pinecrest-faq][^wiki-textile]
- Mill sheets distinguish "White for printing" cloth from plain-dyed, with slightly lower stretch
  (see [stretch properties](/domain/fabrics/stretch-properties.md)).[^notes-fab]

# Constraints

| Constraint | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Polyester base only | Funki; Pine Crest[^funki-bases][^pinecrest-faq] |
| Coloured base restricts inks | See [rule: sublimation ink on coloured base](/domain/fabrics/rule-sublimation-ink-on-coloured-base.md)[^gk-p130] |
| Reorder colour drift | "GK will match the color on reorders to the best of our ability, but cannot guarantee a perfect match. ... wear and washing throughout the season will affect the color saturation"[^gk-p129] |
| 10% tolerance | "Due to the nature of sublimation printing, colour and placement can vary by up to 10 percent, which is an accepted industry tolerance"[^quatro-p117] |
| Banding | Fine lines or banding can appear in digital prints, especially in shaded or solid areas[^funki-faq] |
| Pilling | Printed polyester pills on rough surfaces (GK, Sylvia P, Velocity)[^notes-fab] |

# Printing onto special bases

Printable foil bases exist in white polyester (Pine Crest Printable Jewels Foil; Funki Zing, Lazer,
Clear, Hologram, Platinum); sublimated mesh is offered by Sylvia P and Quatro; The Lineup sells white
crushed velvet, glitter, athletic and pop mesh as sublimation canvases; Funki's foiled net can shift
colour with certain inks.[^pinecrest-printable-jewels][^funki-bases][^quatro-p119][^lineup-novelty]
Foil can also be applied after printing ("AVAILABLE ON ALL DIGITAL PRINTS").[^funki-foilme] An ombré
mystique leotard is printable foil (80% PES / 20% spandex, 200 gsm) with an "Ombre finish graduating
from silver to green".[^velocity-ombre] **[INFERRED]** So print and foil are independent layers in the
material model, with sparkle sitting over the printed colour.[^notes-fab]

# Gaps

- No source states that white cannot be printed, but none lists a white ink. **[INFERRED]** Treat
  white in artwork as unprinted base cloth.[^notes-fab]
- No sublimation gamut or out-of-gamut colour list for polyester-elastane was found.[^notes-fab]
- "Grin-through" (white base showing when a printed knit stretches) is not described or quantified
  anywhere found; nearest evidence is that a one-side-printed net shows colour through to the
  reverse.[^notes-fab] See [stretch-dependent appearance](/engineering/rendering/fabric-shading/stretch-dependent-appearance.md).
- GK does not publish SubFuse, Holotek or Rib composition or weight.[^notes-fab]
