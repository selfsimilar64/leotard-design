# Question

Who decides how a line is made, the designer or the pattern maker, when, and by what reasoning? The
[decision guide](/domain/construction/builds-for-one-look.md) is our stand-in; no maker has confirmed it.[^report]

# Three inexpensive checks that would settle most of it

1. **Talk to one working designer** about this question.
2. **Turn two or three real competition leotards inside out**: one with a plate on mesh, one with a plate under mesh, one with a strap back. No brand says how it builds invisible binding, set-in neckbands, back twists, accent lines or under-mesh plates.
3. **Give three to five designers the plate task** with only the mode bar's prompt to guide them. No source tests picking areas on a body with non-engineers, and it is unknown whether designers read a plate as an area or instinctively want to outline it.
