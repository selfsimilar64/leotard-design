# Question

# Options

1. **Printable PDF**, full scale, tiled for home/office printers and as one large sheet for copy
   shops, with markings and a measurements table.
2. **DXF** (the garment-CAD exchange format) for factories and cutting tables.
3. **Both.**

Also open: whether output covers one size or a graded size run, and whether negative ease is
applied by the tool (per fabric) or left to the maker. See [/domain/construction/](/domain/construction/about.md).
