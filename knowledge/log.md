# Bundle update log

## 2026-10-21
* **Update**: [Realism upgrades](/engineering/rendering/realism-upgrades-at-60fps.md): only the scene is multisampled now (every frame about a third cheaper: B9 35 -> 23 ms at 1792x1344); screen-space skin scattering and half-resolution GTAO on the scene's own depth, both switchable in Display. Body material independent of the head ([Genesis 9 bodies](/engineering/pipeline/g9-bodies.md)): realistic Daz skin or any mannequin finish, plus a mannequin-head finish. Owner reviewed face-abstraction samples (Laplacian smoothing of the face) and rejected the idea; the mannequin head stays as it was.
* **Creation**: [Genesis 9 bodies](/engineering/pipeline/g9-bodies.md): the app now runs on Daz Genesis 9 subdivided once (100,676 vertices) instead of the MakeHuman mannequin, as the owner asked before anything else. Daz Studio bakes 8 body types (Growing Up ages and adult builds) in the A-pose, 8 Skillful Routine poses and the old arm presets one arm at a time (aimed by a Gauss-Newton solve in Daz Script), plus 4 expressions; the app combines body type, pose, expression and head (`none`, `mannequin`: the bald head smoothed, `person`: face and Eirgrid hair) at run time. Designs saved on MakeHuman convert on load; blocks, the POC's fields and `{ p }` / `{ front }` points keep working through the "design frame". Pitfall: a bone turned by Daz Script does not refresh the mesh cache (`forceCacheUpdate`). [Daz bodies](/engineering/pipeline/daz-bodies.md) and [figure scenes](/engineering/pipeline/daz-figures.md) deprecated; [feasibility](/engineering/architecture/genesis9-as-canonical-mesh.md) marked done; [poses](/engineering/app/poses-and-shading.md), [scripting surface](/engineering/app/scripting-surface.md), [document model](/engineering/architecture/design-document-model.md) updated.
* **Update**: [Poses and shading](/engineering/app/poses-and-shading.md): the black flicker while orbiting was dynamic resolution resizing (and so clearing) the canvas after the frame was drawn; now before. Display settings (resolution, anti-aliasing, shadows, glow, lower resolution while moving) per machine.
* **Creation**: [Daz figure scenes](/engineering/pipeline/daz-figures.md): real-person renders. Daz Studio 6 is scripted to bake Genesis 9 with skin, Growing Up age, Skillful Routine pose and expression; the app renders the face, eyes, teeth, lashes and brows (wrapped-diffuse skin, per-vertex occlusion bake, stencilled eye film) with procedural competition hair (slicked back into a bun with a scrunchie, on the Pixie hair's scalp cap) and fits the leotard through the shared Genesis 9 topology. Six showcase scenes, ages 6 to adult. Owner judged the procedural hair "so bad"; Daz's own hairs (Pixie strands as ribbons, Eirgrid cards) now load per scene and are carried onto each head shape (followers and hairs do not follow Growing Up or character head morphs in Daz's export). [Daz bodies](/engineering/pipeline/daz-bodies.md): morph loader reads only Daz's own morphs (Growing Up ships compressed files with neutralizer formulas).
