# Mannequins, dress forms and leotard fit — reference notes

Researched 2026-10-15 for the three.js leotard prototype (MakeHuman-based female mannequins at ages ~8 / ~13 / ~18 / ~30; full-body mannequin and torso dress form; skin-tight leotard).

Conventions: inch figures from sources are converted to cm by me (1 in = 2.54 cm, rounded). **[INFERRED]** marks things I reasoned out or know from general practice but did not find in a source. Several vendor pages (Archiexpo/Best Mannequins, Displays2Go, Morplan, chalkbucket, GK's PDF chart, PGM's chart image) blocked fetching or kept their charts in images, so some cells come from search-result snippets of those pages. Those are noted.

## 1. Full-body fashion / retail mannequins

**Takeaway:** The default modern display mannequin is fibreglass with a **matte white** finish and an **abstract egg (oval) head** or no head. It stands on a **round tempered-glass base plate, ~16 in (41 cm) across for adults and ~14 in (35 cm) for children**. A steel rod rises from the plate into the **calf** (sometimes there is also a spigot into the **foot**). Adult display mannequins are about **180 cm tall with a 86-61-91 cm figure**, much taller and slimmer than real women.

### Facts
- **Head styles.** Abstract heads are common, and the same body is often offered with an oval head, an abstract head or no head. Some makers ship a standard egg head plus an optional rotating semi-abstract head ([search summary of Best Mannequins / Fixtures & Displays listings](https://www.archiexpo.com/prod/best-mannequins-bv/product-4655965-2566567.html)). The "Eve" line is described as an "Abstract Female Mannequin with Egg Head" ([Fixtures & Displays, now redirects to Econoco](https://www.fixturesanddisplays.com/eve-abstract-female-mannequin-with-egg-head-pose-1-of-6.html)). Mannequins became abstract and lost their facial features in the 1970s, and semi-abstract heads are coming back ([FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/mannequins-history-trends-and-key-figures/2016112522618)).
- **Finish and colour.** Matte white is the most common option ("Matte True White"). Other options are white high gloss and matte grey, sometimes with a scratch-resistant finish. Skin tones (matte, or metallic such as bronze) cost about €150 extra. The material is usually fibreglass-reinforced polyester (FRP) ([search summary of Archiexpo/Best Mannequins and Fixtures & Displays listings](https://www.archiexpo.com/prod/best-mannequins-bv/product-4655965-2562756.html)). Best Mannequins' "Atlantic" egg-head model is listed as matte, "milk white" ([Archiexpo](https://www.archiexpo.com/prod/best-mannequins-bv/product-4655965-2559458.html), via search snippet). Cheap polypropylene mannequins are also sold in black and colours ([Walmart FDW listings](https://www.walmart.com/ip/296236300)).
- **Hands and feet.** Hand styles vary: "realistic" hands, "slightly detailed fingers", or articulated fingers. Hands are often detachable or twist-lock at the wrist so the mannequin is easier to dress ([search summary, Fixtures & Displays "Gene" / Best Mannequins](https://www.fixturesanddisplays.com/gene-abstract-seated-male-mannequin-white-pose-5-of-5.html)). Econoco's City Kid line lists "realistic hand and foot style" ([WebstaurantStore](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/econoco-city-kid-12-year-old-female-mannequin-cityk8g/317CITYK8G.html)). **[INFERRED]** On abstract and egg-head lines, the fingers are usually sculpted together with the thumb separate and nails are not modelled. Feet are a smooth, shoe-less "Barbie" foot, often slightly arched for heels, with no toes or only lightly suggested toes.
- **Pose.** Early display mannequins came in three poses: right foot forward, left foot forward, or feet together. Makers release 6–12 new poses per collection ([FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/mannequins-history-trends-and-key-figures/2016112522618)). Product lines are sold as numbered poses, e.g. "Pose 1 of 6", with variants such as "right leg bent", "left leg forward" and "hand on hip" ([Econoco Amber variants](https://webstaurant.com/econoco-amber-plus-size-female-oval-head-mannequin-with-right-leg-bent-amberpl/317AMBERPL.html); [City Kid 10 "with Hand on Hip"](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/econoco-city-kid-12-year-old-female-mannequin-cityk8g/317CITYK8G.html)).
- **Support.** The Eve mannequin has a "16" round tempered glass base" (8 mm thick polished safety glass) with "two fittings for calf and foot" ([search summary of the Fixtures & Displays Eve listing](https://www.fixturesanddisplays.com/eve-abstract-female-mannequin-with-egg-head-pose-5-of-6.html)). Econoco Amber also has a 16 in round tempered glass base ([WebstaurantStore](https://webstaurant.com/econoco-amber-plus-size-female-oval-head-mannequin-with-right-leg-bent-amberpl/317AMBERPL.html)). Children's mannequins have a 13¾ in (35 cm) glass base plus a calf support rod ([City Kid 12F](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/econoco-city-kid-12-year-old-female-mannequin-cityk8g/317CITYK8G.html), [City Kid 8](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/econoco-city-kid-8-year-old-unisex-mannequin-cityk5/317CITYK5.html)). Best Mannequins Atlantic has a "tempered glass round base equipped with a support rod under the foot and at the calf" ([Archiexpo](https://www.archiexpo.com/prod/best-mannequins-bv/product-4655965-2559458.html), via search snippet). Cheap plastic child mannequins use a metal square base instead ([Eddie's](https://www.eddies.com/products/child-or-teen-pre-teen-mannequin-white)). **No rod diameter was found. [INFERRED]** Typical rods are 10–12 mm steel, and they enter the back of the calf about 25–35 cm above the floor.
- **Adult display proportions.** A typical female mannequin is 5'11" (180 cm), dress size 4–6, 34B bust, 24–25 in waist (61–64 cm) and 36 in hips (91 cm). That is about 6 in taller than the average woman ([FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/mannequins-history-trends-and-key-figures/2016112522618)). Another commonly cited figure is about 6 ft with a 34-24-34 in figure (86-61-86 cm) ([Refinery29 citing the Guardian](https://refinery29.com/en-us/store-mannequins-womens-body-shape)). Retailers say thinner mannequins are easier to dress and that clothes hang better on them ([same](https://refinery29.com/en-us/store-mannequins-womens-body-shape)).

### Implications for our 3D model
- Material presets: **matte white** (roughness ~0.6–0.7, warm-white albedo ~#EDEAE4) is the default. Offer **gloss white** (roughness ~0.15, clearcoat) and **matte black** as alternatives. **[INFERRED values]**
- Head: smooth **egg-shaped ovoid** with no features, about 22 cm tall for an adult, plus a "headless" toggle that ends in a flat neck cap. Do not use the MakeHuman face. **[INFERRED proportions]**
- Hands: merge the fingers into one mitten with a separate thumb. Feet: smooth, no toes. **[INFERRED]**
- Pose: near-A-pose, with arms relaxed about 10–15° out from the body and slightly bent, and weight on one leg with the other knee slightly forward (classic "Pose 1"). This keeps the leotard's side seams and armholes visible. **[INFERRED]**
- Base: round **clear/tinted glass disc**, **Ø 40.6 cm × 0.8 cm** for adult and teen mannequins and **Ø 35 cm** for the child. Add a chrome **Ø ~1.2 cm rod** from the plate, behind the standing leg, into the back of the calf about 30 cm up. **[rod size INFERRED]**
- Our mannequins are built from real-body MakeHuman proportions, so they will look shorter and fuller than retail mannequins. That is the right choice for a fit tool, but note it in the UI.

## 3. Torso dress forms (dressmaking, swim, body forms)

**Takeaway:** A professional form is a **fibreglass or papier-mâché shell**, padded with felt or foam and covered in **linen or canvas** with **raised or marked style lines**. It has **collapsible or clip-on shoulder caps (no arms)** and a **cast-iron or aluminium caster base with a foot-pedal height pole**. Standard bodices end at the **hip or upper thigh**. "3/4" or "half-leg" forms, used for swimwear, add **thighs cut at mid-thigh** and a real crotch. No leotard-specific form was found; swim forms are the closest match.

### Facts
- **Construction.** The shell is thick fibreglass bonded with resin in two side moulds. A steel bar runs from the top to about the waist. Needle-wool padding is glued on, then linen goes over it as a smoothing layer, and a "measurement line" is added during finishing ([Wikipedia: Dress form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_form)). PGM uses "100% Linen with high density felt (not sponge)", raised side seams, collapsible shoulders, an aluminium base and a foot-pedal height adjustment; arms are sold separately ([PGM Missy form](https://www.pgmdressform.com/Professional-Female-Dress-Form-Missy-Dress-Forms-Fashion-Class)). Shop Co forms have a fibreglass core with felt and a canvas exterior, "integrated seam lines", removable clip-in shoulders ("shoulders not arms"), and a cast-iron base with pedal height adjustment ([Shop Co child form](https://theshopcompany.com/professional-child-3-4-body-dressform-with-removable%20arms.html)). Stockman forms are papier-mâché covered in off-white cotton, and antique ones have a turned wooden finial on top ([search summary, 1stdibs](https://www.1stdibs.com/buy/stockman-mannequin)). Wolf forms have a linen pinnable body, metal finishings, adjustable height and a cast-iron base ([U. Alberta collection record](https://clothingtextiles.ualberta.ca/2018/06/07/off-white-size-10-collapsible-dress-form/)).
- **Style lines and tape.** Tape goes around the bust, waist, neckline and hip lines. Princess lines run from mid-shoulder (front and back) over the bust point and then straight down ([Threads forum](https://www.threadsmagazine.com/forum/where-to-put-twill-tape-on-my-dress-form)). Draping guides also mark centre front, centre back, armscye, cross shoulder and neckline ([search summary](https://loosethreads.substack.com/p/3-lines-of-demarcation)).
- **Bottom cut.** PGM's "Industry Grade" line has legs, and the pole can go through the centre or through a leg. It is measured to max thigh, mid thigh, knee and so on ([PGM](https://www.pgmdressform.com/DRESS-FORMS-PGM-Professional-Dress-Form-USA-for-Fashion-Design)). Shop Co 3/4 forms are a "half-leg body" for rompers and swimsuits ([adult](https://theshopcompany.com/products/professional-female-3-4-body-dress-form-soft-foam-fully-pinnable)). Panache's "Bloomsbury" 3/4 torso: "neck finishes just below the head and legs finish mid thigh" ([Morplan, search snippet](https://www-morplan-com-22657.36.mdoq.io/gb_en/pdbs1)). Best Mannequins COUTF13 is a "dress form with legs" cut at mid-thigh, with a removable head and neck block ([Archiexpo, search snippet](https://www.archiexpo.com/prod/best-mannequins-bv/product-4655965-2564740.html)). PGM measures abdomen at 4 in (10 cm) and hip at 8 in (20 cm) below the waist ([PGM](https://www.pgmdressform.com/)).
- **Display forms.** These sit on round metal or wood bases, wood tripods or rolling bases ([Dress Forms USA](https://dressformsusa.com/)). Child display forms use a wood flat base ([Shop Co](https://theshopcompany.com/products/child-display-dress-form-on-wood-flat-base)).
- **Swim forms.** Barr Display sells a fibreglass "Brazilian swimsuit torso form" in natural tan ([search snippet](https://www-barrdisplay-com-21978.30.mdoq.io/mannequins-display-forms/specialty-mannequins/female-brazilian-swimsuit-torso-form-large-busted-mannequin.html)). Fixtures & Displays sells a fleshtone activewear counter-top torso, about 30–36 in tall ([search snippet](https://www.fixturesanddisplays.com/ladies-activewear-counter-top-torso-form-fleshtone.html)). The Alvanon swim form is a full torso with a widened crotch ([Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/business-news/retail/alvanon-brings-intimateswim-form-for-women/)).
- **Not found:** neck-cap diameter and material specs, pole diameter, base footprint. **[INFERRED from general product knowledge]** Neck caps are a turned wood or metal disc about 9–11 cm across, sitting 4–6 cm above the neck base. Poles are about 2.5–3 cm steel tube with a collar. Caster bases are 3–5 legs spanning 50–60 cm. Standard bodice forms end about 5–10 cm below the hip line, flat-bottomed with a rim.

### Implications for our 3D model
- **Dress form = MakeHuman body cut.** Cut the neck about **5 cm above the neck base**, with a flat top and a **Ø 10 cm, 1.5 cm thick turned-wood cap** (walnut). **[INFERRED]**
- Shoulders: cut the arms flush at the **armscye plane** and cap them with smooth rounded shoulder caps. No arm stubs.
- Because the leotard needs a crotch and leg openings, **use the swim-form "3/4 / half-leg" cut: legs cut horizontally at mid-thigh**, i.e. about 50% of the way from crotch to knee, roughly 12–15 cm below the crotch for an adult and 8–10 cm for the child. Close the cut with a flat disc or a slightly domed cap. **[ratio INFERRED; "mid-thigh" sourced]**
- Covering: matte **natural linen/oatmeal** (#D9CDB8, roughness 0.9, subtle weave normal map). Add thin dark **style-line tape (~6 mm)** at bust, waist, hip, centre front/back, princess lines and side seams. **[colour/width INFERRED]**
- Stand: **Ø 2.5 cm chrome pole** from the crotch-cap centre (as on PGM), a collar or height knob, and a **5-leg caster base about 55 cm across** (pro form). Alternatively, use a 3-leg wood tripod (display style). **[INFERRED]**

## 4. How a gymnastics/dance leotard sits on the body

**Takeaway:** The rulebook sets the limits. The **leg opening may rise no higher than the hip bone** on the side; aerobic rules say the outside line runs **through the iliac crest** and not above the waist. The **front neckline stops at mid-sternum or higher**, the **back neckline at the lower edge of the shoulder blades or higher**, and **straps are at least 2 cm wide**. The fabric is cut **about 10% smaller than the body** (finished garments run 20–25% negative ease).

### Facts — rules and cut
- WAG (women's artistic): "neckline… no further than half of the sternum and no further than the lower line of the shoulder blades"; "the leg cut of the leotard may not extend beyond the hip bone"; straps must be at least 2 cm wide; sleeves are optional; leg length (unitard/shorts) ends no more than 2 cm below the base of the buttocks ([GymCastic summary of WAG CoP](https://gymcastic.com/will-i-lose-my-gold-medal-because-of-my-leotard-uncovering-elite-gymnastics-rules/)). Same wording on a forum reproduction ([chalkbucket](https://chalkbucket.com/threads/leotard-regulations.40056/latest), search snippet). The official 2025–28 WAG text was not retrieved.
- FIG Aerobic: "Leg cut may not rise above waist height"; the outside seam must pass through the "crest of ilium"; the leotard must cover the crotch completely; the neckline limits are the same as WAG ([FIG Aerobic CoP 3.1](https://www.fig-aerobic.com/3-1-DRESS-CODE_a1591.html)).
- Ballet "leg line" cuts sit higher on the hip to lengthen the leg line ([Capezio](https://www.capezio.com/pages/shop-ballet-leg-line-black-leotards)).
- Drafting a high-cut brief: the side seams angle in toward the waist, and you choose the side-seam length by marking the top edge and the leg-top on the body and measuring between them along the side ([Sewing With Numbers](https://sewingwithnumbers.substack.com/p/how-to-draft-high-cut-briefs)). Leotards are usually traced from a fitted suit, with ½ in seam allowance at the crotch, sides and shoulders and ¼ in at the neckline and leg openings, which are finished with elastic ([search summary of mybluprint / Peekaboo tutorials](https://peekaboopatternshop.com/our-blog/how-to-sew-a-leotard)).
- **No source gave numeric leg-line rise or side-seam length for girls' sizes. [INFERRED from the rules plus typical GK/competition garments]:**
  - *Side:* on competition leotards the leg line peaks at or just below the **iliac crest**. Side-seam length from underarm to leg opening is roughly **45–55% of the armpit-to-crotch distance**, about 9–12 cm for an adult and 6–8 cm for an 8-year-old.
  - *Front:* the opening runs from that high point down and inward to cross the **inguinal crease (thigh–torso fold)** about 2–4 cm above where it meets the crotch. The gusset at the crotch is **5–6 cm wide (adult) / 4 cm (child)**.
  - *Back:* "full back" coverage on gymnastics leotards covers most of the gluteus. The leg line runs from the side peak down to the **gluteal fold about 1/3 of the way in from the side**, so the lower outer buttock is exposed. Dance "cheeky" cuts expose more.
  - *Neckline:* a typical gymnastics scoop sits **8–12 cm below the suprasternal notch (adult), well above mid-sternum**. The back scoop sits at or above **mid-scapula**. Long-sleeve competition leotards often use a high round neck about 1–2 cm below the neck base. Tank armholes are cut about **2–3 cm below the axilla**, with the strap or shoulder edge 3–5 cm wide at the shoulder. Long-sleeve armholes follow the natural armscye.
- **Seams [INFERRED, standard construction]:** side seams (sometimes replaced by princess/panel seams on competition leos), shoulder seams, crotch/gusset seam, centre-back seam on some styles, and set-in sleeve seams on long sleeves. Edges have elastic turned under and coverstitched (about **8–10 mm** wide) or are bound (**~1 cm**).

### Facts — stretch and ease
- A typical 80/20 nylon–spandex 4-way stretch is cut to about **90% of the body measurement** ("10% pattern reduction"); worked example: 28 in waist → 25¼ in pattern ([search summary of swim-sewing guides](https://www.seamwork.com/articles/sew-the-maggie-swimsuit)). Swimsuits run **20–25% negative ease**, and athletic suits (Speedo/Nike) up to **30%** ([search summary, Threads/Cashmerette](https://blog.cashmerette.com/2019/02/what-is-negative-ease-and-how-does-it-work.html)). Horizontal and vertical stretch ratios can be measured separately ([Seamly forum](https://forum.seamly.io/t/swimsuit-lingerie-patterns/16915)).
- Leotards are deliberately negative ease, "snug, close-fitting, supportive" ([gymnasticsleotards.co.uk](https://www.gymnasticsleotards.co.uk/pages/sizing)). Competition leos are often a size smaller than practice ones ([chalkbucket via search summary](https://chalkbucket.com/threads/alpha-factor-vs-gk-sizing.67723)). Torso length decides whether a leotard "rides up or digs in" ([GMD Direct](https://www.gmdirect.com.au/blogs/everything-artistic-gymnastics/how-to-measure-your-gymnast-at-home)). Destira runs "long and skinny" compared with GK ([chalkbucket via search summary](https://chalkbucket.com/threads/destira-leotards.43013/latest)).
- **Visual behaviour [INFERRED, no source found]:** taut fabric **bridges concavities**: the inter-gluteal cleft, the spine groove, the navel, the under-bust fold and the hollow between the breasts are spanned with a flat surface rather than followed. Soft tissue is compressed about 2–5 mm (more at bust and buttock). The bust is flattened and rounded into a single "uni-bust" shape. Elastic edges sit **1–2 mm into the skin**, with a slight bulge of skin just outside the leg and arm openings on adults (little on children). There are almost no wrinkles except small tension lines from the shoulder toward the crotch, and a few horizontal folds at the lower back when the body is arched.

### Implications for our 3D model
- **Size assignment:** child 8 → **CM** (torso ≈ 120); teen 13 → **AXS** (≈ 134); young adult 18 → **AS** (≈ 141); adult 30 → **AS/AM** (≈ 145). Check by measuring the mesh's mid-shoulder→crotch→mid-shoulder loop.
- **Fit solve:** shrink the garment to **90% of body girth** horizontally and about **92–95% vertically**, then project onto the body with a small offset (fabric thickness **0.6–0.8 mm**). In the concave regions listed above, replace the body surface with its **convex-hull-like smoothed surface** (e.g. Laplacian-smoothed offset, or "shrink-wrap then relax"). **[INFERRED]**
- **Soft-tissue compression:** displace the body inward by **2–3 mm** under the fabric, **4–5 mm** at bust and buttocks on adults, and 1–2 mm on the child. Add a **1–1.5 mm groove** in the body under each elastic edge, about 8 mm wide. **[INFERRED]**
- **Leg line defaults (adjustable):** side peak at **iliac crest height minus 1 cm** (competition high-cut), with a "moderate" preset at about the midpoint between crest and crotch. Front crosses the inguinal crease, gusset 5.5 cm (adult) / 4 cm (child). Back covers about 2/3 of the buttock at "full back". Neck scoop 10 cm below the sternal notch (adult; scale by height for the child). Back scoop at mid-scapula. Tank strap 4 cm at the shoulder. All **[INFERRED within sourced rule limits]**.
- Seams to render: side seams, shoulder seams, crotch seam, optional princess seams. Edges: **9 mm** elastic/coverstitch band with a slight sheen difference. **[INFERRED]**
