# Takeaway

> Deferred: rule checking is out of scope since 2026-10-13
> ([decision](/decisions/2026-10-13-defer-competition-rules-and-pain-points.md)).

For the 2025-2028 cycle only two things are **verified** from current text: the **neckline/back
depth limit** (front no lower than half-way down the sternum; back no lower than the lower line of
the shoulder blades) as reproduced by USA Gymnastics for its elite meets,[^usag-elite] and the
separate [clothing, emblem and advertising rules](/domain/rules/fig-advertising-and-identification.md).
The full attire article (Art. 2.3.2) and its deduction table were **not retrieved**.[^styles]

# Verified rules

| Rule | Limit | Status / source |
|---|---|---|
| Neckline depth, front | No lower than half of the sternum | Verified, current wording via USA Gymnastics Elite Rules 4.e[^usag-elite] |
| Neckline depth, back | No lower than the lower line of the shoulder blades | Verified, same[^usag-elite] |
| Garment type | Correct, sportive, **non-transparent** leotard or unitard of elegant design; unitard = one-piece with full-length legs, hip to ankle; complete leg coverings in the leotard's colour allowed, under or over | Wording quoted in 2021 from the then-current Code; not re-verified for 2025-2028[^sin-2021] |
| Head coverings | Permitted if tight-fitting, face exposed, same colour(s) as the leotard | Verified in the clothing rules (Appendix E)[^styles] |

# Where the article lives

WAG Code of Points 2025-2028 places attire at **Article 2.3.2** (Section 2, gymnast's duties), per
a search snippet of a German judging site's recording-sheet header.[^kari-header] The Code was
approved by the FIG Executive Committee in February 2024; the German site lists an English original
dated 23 May 2025; FIG's February 2026 newsletter carries replacement pages marked November 2025
and has **no attire changes**.[^scribd-cop][^kari-index][^fig-nl-2026] The official PDF exceeded
the 10 MB fetch limit.[^fig-cop]

# Unverified recollections (leads, not findings)

From memory of the previous (2022-2024) edition, and repeated by a secondary summary:[^styles][^gymcastic]

- Shoulder straps at least **2 cm** wide **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Leg cut not beyond the hip bone; leg length (unitard/shorts) not lower than about **2 cm below the
  base of the buttocks** **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Identical leotards for one federation's gymnasts in qualification and team final; no jewellery
  except small studs; no hip or other padding; bandages beige or skin-coloured **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Deductions: **0.30** from the final score for incorrect attire, missing emblem, missing number or
  incorrect advertising; **1.00** from the team for non-identical leotards **[UNVERIFIED]**. New
  Zealand's 0.3 and 1.00 penalties mirror this structure, which suggests but does not prove FIG's
  figures are the same.[^styles]

The mannequins note quotes the same strap and leg rules from the GymCastic summary as if
settled;[^fit] treat them as unverified until the 2025-2028 Code is read.

# Other FIG disciplines (contrast)

Rhythmic requires non-transparent material from chest to crotch with the same half-sternum and
shoulder-blade limits (search snippets);[^fig-rg] aerobic requires the crotch covered up to the hip
bones in non-transparent, non-skin-coloured material, the leg cut not above waist height, and the
outside seam passing through the crest of the ilium.[^fig-aerobic][^fit]

# Open

Whether nude-lined mesh above the half-sternum line satisfies the transparency and neckline tests
is not addressed anywhere found. See [unverified rules](/domain/rules/unverified-and-missing-rules.md).
