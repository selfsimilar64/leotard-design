# Takeaway

> Deferred: rule checking is out of scope since 2026-10-13
> ([decision](/decisions/2026-10-13-defer-competition-rules-and-pain-points.md)).

USA Gymnastics (Development and Xcel, effective 1 Aug 2026) bans **backless, racerback and
spaghetti-strap** leotards and **bare midriffs**, and requires the **leg cut below the
pelvis/hip bone**. Any attire infraction costs **0.20 after an initial warning**.[^usag-rp] The
backless test is geometric and exempts openings filled with mesh or flesh-coloured fabric, so it
can be checked on a 3D back view **[INFERRED]**.[^styles]

# Appropriate

- Full-sleeve and sleeveless leotards, leg cut below the pelvis/hip bone.
- Unitards with ankle- or short-length legs, with or without sleeves.
- Tights (capri or longer) and shorts, under or over the leotard, in black, leotard-matching or
  skin tone, no embellishment beyond small manufacturer branding.
- Gymnastics footwear; bra straps in black, leotard-matching, skin tone or clear, with no underwear
  deliberately exposed; stud earrings anywhere in the ear, other piercings removed.[^usag-rp]

# Inappropriate

| Item | Definition |
|---|---|
| Backless leotard | Open area visually extends **more than about 2 inches below the bottom of the shoulder blade**, or **wider than the vertical midpoint of the shoulder blade**, unless the open area is filled with mesh or flesh-coloured fabric[^usag-rp] |
| Racerback leotard | Banned (named type)[^usag-rp] |
| Spaghetti straps | Banned; no numeric width given[^usag-rp] |
| Bare midriff, T-shirts, boxer or loose shorts, tennis shoes | Banned[^usag-rp] |

Deduction: **0.20** after a warning; the Xcel Code of Points also sets incorrect attire at
0.20.[^usag-rp][^nawgj-xcel] A missing competitor number at Level 9 Easterns/Westerns or Level 10
Nationals is treated as an attire deduction after a warning (Ch. 7).[^usag-rp]

GK's customer restatement (Sept 2026): leg opening not cut or rolled above the hip; no bare midriff;
a back opening beyond the shoulder-blade midpoint and more than two inches past the shoulder blades
is backless; racerback and spaghetti straps prohibited; undergarments hidden.[^gk-dresscode]

# History

Ankle-length tights under or over the leotard recommended May 2022; November 2022 gave racerbacks
an extra year's grace while other backless leotards became subject to 0.20; bra-strap colour
allowance passed the National Technical Committee in May 2025 for 2025-26.[^nawgj-xcel][^usag-tc-2025]
No 2026-27 attire change was identified (update decks are slide PDFs that may carry text as
images).[^styles]

# Elite programme

International elite competitions in the US follow FIG rules; US elite meets apply the FIG neckline
rule (half-sternum front, lower shoulder-blade line back); athletes wear club apparel in the arena
at Winter Cup, American Classic, U.S. Classic and U.S. Championships.[^usag-elite] See
[FIG attire rules](/domain/rules/fig-attire-rules.md).

# Not stated

The table names full-sleeve and sleeveless leotards only; three-quarter sleeves are universal in
practice and sold as compliant, but no rule text names them. No team-uniformity rule was found in
the Development/Xcel table.[^styles] Source and placement: Rules & Policies, Ch. 2, B.4, p. 2.[^usag-rp][^usag-rules-page]
