# Takeaway

The 2010s were defined by strappy backs, varied necklines and ever more rhinestones; the 2020s by
nude mesh, sublimated ombré, velvet's return, matte fabrics replacing shiny ones, the unitard as a
legitimate elite option, and crystal counts roughly doubling at Olympic level (about 5,000 in 2016
to about 10,000 on the 2024 team-final leotard).[^styles] For what is current now, see
[current trends](/domain/garment/current-trends.md).

| Era | Markers |
|---|---|
| 1980s (NCAA) | Bold colours, stripes, spandex[^ncaa-com-2024] |
| 1990s (NCAA) | Crushed velvet, colour blocks, large side panels, high collars, keyhole cut-outs, first rhinestones and metallics, the matching scrunchie[^ncaa-com-2024] |
| 2000s (NCAA) | Swirls, mesh, metallics, asymmetry, single mesh sleeves (Alabama 2009); embellishment judged by how it looked on a CRT television[^ncaa-com-2024] |
| 2010s (NCAA) | Varied silhouettes, strappy designs, rhinestones wherever possible; skyline and landmark graphics (Utah); Arizona State's spaghetti-strap `Pitchfork`[^ncaa-com-2024] |
| Rio 2016 | US Olympic leotards carried nearly 5,000 crystals each[^marieclaire-2024] |
| 2021 (Tokyo cycle) | German women in ankle-length unitards at the Europeans and Olympics; Michigan won the NCAA title in a velvet leotard with a cult following[^sin-2021][^ncaa-com-2024] |
| 2020s (NCAA) | Nude mesh, ombré, even more rhinestones; custom designs in every cut, colour and fabric, driven by NIL-era branding[^ncaa-com-2024] |
| Paris 2024 (US, GK Elite) | Eight women's leotards, more than 47,000 Swarovski crystals in total; team-final `Go for Glory` had 9,929 crystals and weighed nearly a pound; Swarovski pearls used for the first time; custom red stretch satin; velvet bindings and collars; ombré bodices; corseting and zig-zag trim; athletes unanimous on red, white and blue[^marieclaire-2024][^nbc-2024][^cornell] |
| Paris 2024 (matte shift) | Diaz: athletes now prefer matte fabrics with crystals; shiny becoming less of a default[^vogue-2024] |
| Paris 2024 (Egypt, Snowflake) | Black foil body with gold and teal foil accents, nearly 7,000 crystals across leotard and scrunchie, crystal snake round the right arm[^insidegym-2024] |

**Crystal tiers [INFERRED]:** about 800-2,900 on a heavily crystalled club leotard, about 5,000 on a
2016 Olympic leotard, about 10,000 on a 2024 team-final leotard.[^styles] See
[/domain/embellishments/](/domain/embellishments/about.md).

**Gaps:** no sourced crystal counts for London 2012 or Tokyo 2020 US leotards; nothing on
2012-2016 Olympic-cycle trends beyond the Rio count. NCAA trend evidence is concentrated in one
outlet and brand copy.[^styles]
