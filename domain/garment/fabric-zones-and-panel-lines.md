# Takeaway

Brands describe a leotard as **five to seven named fabric zones**: body (or upper and lower body),
sleeves, body panels or inserts, belt, binding and straps, plus underlays or overlays. Simple
designs use three zones (body, sleeves, binding); elaborate catalogue designs six or seven plus
layered mesh.[^styles] Design lines are curved or asymmetric panel seams, sweetheart or V overlays,
belts, bindings and printed (sublimated) gradients. **Wherever mesh sits on the torso it is lined in
a skin tone; sleeves stay unlined.**[^sylviap-cat][^styles]

# Zone vocabulary

- Sylvia P Studio SP: `Body`, `Inserts & Sleeves`, `Belt`, `Lower Body`, `Straps & Binding`,
  `Embellishments`.[^sylviap-sibella]
- Sylvia P fabrication notes: `Main body`, `Upper body`, `Lower body`, `Upper body underlay panels`,
  `Mesh overlay`, `Gathered overlay panels`, `Body panels` / `mesh body panels`, `Accent panel lines`,
  `Accent underlay body panel`, `Belt`, `Sleeves`, `Opposite sleeve`, `Neck binding`, `Neckband`,
  `Invisible binding`, `Contrast binding`, `rouleau straps`, `Back twists`, `Back twist underlay`.
  Individual designs name three to seven distinct fabric zones.[^sylviap-cat]

# Design-line types

- Sylvia P 2026-27: asymmetric body paneling with a waist seam; pointed panel lines wrapping body
  and waist; curved mesh paneling; a curved princess line followed by crystal lines; sweetheart V
  paneling; colour-block bodice with belt paneling; V-shaped belt line; twisting panel lines; mesh
  cut-outs; mystique appliqué; double-layer wrap illusion.[^sylviap-cat]
- GK: corseting look, zig-zag trim, ruching at the waist (spelled `Rouching`), sublimated top with
  solid bottom, wrap mesh detailing, contrast binding, mesh cut-outs.[^gk-9661s][^gk-special]

# Mesh placement

Sleeves and upper bodice (elite standard per Diaz); shoulders (GK `mesh sleeves and shoulders`;
Quatro shoulder accents); side panels (Quatro sublimated mesh); a single mesh-filled waist cut-out
(Illinois, Minnesota, Michigan State, Florida in 2026); upper back; keyhole infill.[^cornell][^gk-special][^quatro-all][^cgn-2026]

# Lining rules

- Sylvia P: all mesh body panels lined with skin lining for zero transparency; the front is always
  fully lined; on light-coloured leotards the lower back or the entire back is lined too;
  competitive leotards lined at no extra cost.[^sylviap-cat][^sylviap-design]
- GK: required mesh bodice lining in `nude shadowproof` on mesh-bodice styles; a separate seamless
  liner (style 1487) recommended under white or light colours (liner note from a search
  snippet).[^gk-special]
- Quatro: three nude tones for lining and mesh.[^quatro-fabrics] See
  [illusion layer](/domain/garment/illusion-layer.md).

# Fabric families and durability

Fabric families filling the zones (GK `SoftLux`, `Mystique`, `SubFuse`, `Primatex`, `Mesh`,
`meshfoil`, sparkle hologram, velvet, stretch satin; Sylvia P `Matt Lycra`, `Mystique`, `Mesh`,
`Fog Mesh`, `Wet Look`, `Diamond Mesh`; Quatro `LYCRA`, `MYSTIQUE`, `MESH`, `SUBLIMATED LYCRA`) are
covered in [/domain/fabrics/](/domain/fabrics/about.md). Durability notes relevant to zone choice:
matt Lycra is the most durable and fades least; mystique can fade and dull where crystals are
heat-applied; meshes snag; sublimated fabrics pill on rough surfaces such as mats, grips and Velcro
(Sylvia P and GK).[^sylviap-fabrics][^sylviap-care][^gk-special]

# Logos and lettering as zone elements

GK embroiders its logo at the left hip on special-order styles; Quatro sublimates a logo on the
hip; Destira presses a star logo on every custom item and allows 8-9 letters per row for gym
names.[^gk-9661s][^quatro-open][^destira-rsc]

Era markers: 1990s crushed-velvet colour blocks and large side panels; 2000s swirls, mesh and
asymmetry; 2020s nude mesh and ombré.[^ncaa-com-2024]

# Implications [INFERRED]

- Panel model mirroring brand zones: upper body, lower body, optional belt, left and right sleeves,
  inserts, binding, straps, plus underlay and overlay layers.[^styles]
- Torso mesh zones get an automatic lining layer with a nude tone; light body colours prompt full
  lining.
- Sublimation makes panels optional: the tool needs seam-defined colour blocking **and**
  print-defined artwork on the same pattern piece.
- Durability flags at design time: sublimated or mesh fabric on the seat pills or snags sooner.
