# Takeaway

**Scoop is the default everywhere.** V, soft V, high neck (with neckband or collar, and a back
opening), square, boatneck-scoop, halter-style high neck and asymmetric/one-shoulder all appear in
2026 catalogues.[^styles] A high, mock or halter-high neck cannot be pulled over the head, so it
**forces a back opening and a closure** (see [constraints](/domain/garment/choice-constraints.md)).
FIG limits the front neckline to no lower than half-way down the sternum and the back to no lower
than the lower line of the shoulder blades.[^usag-elite]

# Finishes and details

- **Collars:** velvet was used on the 2024 US Olympic leotards for binding and high-neck collars,
  kept to small areas to avoid overheating.[^cornell] NCAA 2026: Oklahoma and New Hampshire used a
  split collar; several schools put their name on the collar; more collar experimentation predicted
  for 2027.[^cgn-2026] In 2025 a reviewer said she was tired of high-neck, thick-collar
  designs.[^cgn-2025]
- **Keyholes and notches at the front:** Quatro Eternity has a front neck notch below a high collar;
  Sylvia P Paige combines small keyholes with sweetheart paneling.[^quatro-ls][^sylviap-cat]
- **Asymmetric:** Auburn's one-shoulder `Bloom` (2026); LSU's 2025 cut-out from the back up to one
  shoulder was criticised as distracting.[^cgn-2026][^cgn-2025]
- **Era note:** NCAA moved from round necks to high collars and keyholes in the 1990s; the 2010s
  brought wide variety.[^ncaa-com-2024]

# Typical placement on the body [INFERRED]

A typical gymnastics scoop sits 8-12 cm below the suprasternal notch on an adult, well above the
mid-sternum limit; long-sleeve competition leotards often use a high round neck 1-2 cm below the
neck base; the back scoop sits at or above mid-scapula.[^fit] See
[how a leotard sits on the body](/domain/sizing/how-a-leotard-sits-on-the-body.md).

# Suggested model [INFERRED]

`shape` (scoop, round, V, soft V, high V, square, boatneck-scoop, high/mock, halter-high, racer,
one-shoulder) + `depth` + `finish` (self binding, contrast binding, invisible binding, neckband of
set width, stand collar, split collar) + optional [illusion layer](/domain/garment/illusion-layer.md).[^styles]

# Gaps

"Choker" not found as a brand term; "boat neck" only as GK's `boatneck scoop neckline`. No rule text
on whether one-shoulder designs are permitted under FIG, USA Gymnastics or NCAA. No brand states
numeric neckline depths or neckband widths.[^styles]
