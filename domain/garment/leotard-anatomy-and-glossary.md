# Takeaway

A leotard is a one-piece, skin-tight, negative-ease garment covering the torso from shoulders to
the crotch, with optional sleeves and optional leg extensions. Brands break it into a body
(often upper and lower body), sleeves, inserts or body panels, a belt, straps and binding, and
underlay or overlay layers.[^sylviap-sibella][^sylviap-cat][^styles] The design vocabulary below is
what designers, factories and catalogues actually say.

# Parts of the garment

| Part | What it is | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| Body / main body | The torso piece; often split into `Upper body` and `Lower body` at a waist or belt line | [fabric zones](/domain/garment/fabric-zones-and-panel-lines.md) |
| Upper bodice | Chest and shoulder area; where mesh and illusion layers usually sit | [illusion layer](/domain/garment/illusion-layer.md) |
| Neckline, neckband, collar | Front and back neck edge; a high neck has a set-in neckband or collar | [necklines](/domain/garment/necklines.md) |
| Armscye (armhole) | Where the sleeve joins, or the finished arm opening on a tank | [sleeves](/domain/garment/sleeves.md) |
| Sleeve, cuff | Cap, short, three-quarter or full; cuff is the sleeve-end band | [sleeves](/domain/garment/sleeves.md) |
| Back opening | Scoop, V, keyhole, open, racerback, strappy | [backs](/domain/garment/backs.md) |
| Closure | Hook clasp at the back neck, or invisible centre-back zip | [closures](/domain/garment/closures.md) |
| Straps | Shoulder straps on tanks; decorative back straps on strappy backs | [straps](/domain/garment/straps.md) |
| Belt / waist line | A horizontal or V-shaped band at the waist | [fabric zones](/domain/garment/fabric-zones-and-panel-lines.md) |
| Leg opening (leg line) | The curve from hip, over the inguinal crease, through the crotch and round the buttock | [leg cut and seat](/domain/garment/leg-cut-and-seat.md) |
| Gusset / crotch | The narrow piece between the legs; about 5-6 cm wide on adults, 4 cm on children **[INFERRED]**[^fit] | [how a leotard sits](/domain/sizing/how-a-leotard-sits-on-the-body.md) |
| Seat | The back lower body; keeping it from riding up is a core fit problem[^styles] | [leg cut and seat](/domain/garment/leg-cut-and-seat.md) |
| Lining | Skin-tone ("nude") fabric behind mesh and light colours | [fabric zones](/domain/garment/fabric-zones-and-panel-lines.md) |

# Trade terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tank | Sleeveless leotard, mainly training wear |
| 3Q / 3/4 sleeve | Three-quarter-length sleeve; the US club competition default |
| Biketard | Leotard with short legs (short-leg unitard) |
| Unitard | One-piece with full-length legs, hip to ankle |
| Illusion / nude mesh | Mesh backed by skin-tone lining so an area reads as skin |
| Sweetheart | A curved, heart-top line, used as an overlay or panel line rather than an open neckline |
| Keyhole | A cut-out opening at the back neck, often the head opening behind a high neck |
| Binding | Narrow strip finishing an edge (neck, armhole, leg); contrast or `invisible binding`[^sylviap-cat] |
| Rouleau | Narrow tube strap cut from matt Lycra[^sylviap-cat] |
| Mystique | Foil-printed shiny stretch fabric; see [/domain/fabrics/](/domain/fabrics/about.md) |
| Sublimation | Dye printing of artwork into polyester-rich fabric; makes printed gradients, ombré and faux straps possible |
| Faux straps | Straps printed on, or laid over nude mesh, instead of real openings |
| Raglan | Sleeve whose seam runs diagonally from underarm to neck instead of round the shoulder |
| Set-in sleeve | Sleeve sewn into a round armscye; assumed default where raglan is not stated[^styles] |
| `nude shadowproof` | GK's required lining fabric under mesh bodices[^gk-special] |
| Torso (girth) / torso loop | Loop from mid-shoulder down the front, through the crotch, up the back to the same shoulder; the primary sizing measurement[^gk-sizechart][^fit] |
| Leg cut: traditional/workout, modified, standard, mid cut | Brand presets for leg-opening height, not geometrically defined |
| Negative ease | Garment smaller than the body; leotards are cut about 10% under body girth[^fit] |
| Seam allowance | Typically 1/2 in at crotch, sides and shoulders and 1/4 in at neck and leg openings, which are finished with elastic[^peekaboo][^fit] |

See [/domain/construction/](/domain/construction/about.md) for how the pieces are cut and sewn.
