# Takeaway

Most leotards have **no closure**: scoop, V and open backs are pulled on.[^gk-4959s][^styles] A
closure appears when the neck is too high to pass the head, and then it is almost always a **hook
clasp at the back neck** above a keyhole; an **invisible centre-back zip** is the rare
alternative.[^styles]

| Closure | Brand wording | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Pull-on (none) | – | Norm for scoop, V and open backs; GK notes a plain scoop back leaves the most room for embellishment[^gk-4959s] |
| Hook clasp at back neck | GK `hook clasp closure`, `hook neck clasp closure`; Sylvia P `Narrow metal clasp at the back neck` and a metal branded clasp; Quatro `20mm back clasp` securing a high collar[^gk-special][^sylviap-cat][^quatro-ls] | Largest back group at GK special order (16-18 of 38 keyhole/clasp); at least 9 of 20 Sylvia P designs; clasp in 67 of 405 Quatro descriptions[^gk-special][^sylviap-cat][^quatro-all] |
| Invisible zip | Sylvia P Kamira: invisible zip at centre back; Quatro Treille: invisible black zip below a high neckline[^sylviap-cat][^quatro-all] | 1 of 20 Sylvia P; zip in 6 of 405 Quatro[^sylviap-cat][^quatro-all] |

Straps on strappy backs usually meet at the back-neck clasp (see [straps](/domain/garment/straps.md)).
An NCAA reviewer praised a delicate neck clasp (Arizona, 2025).[^cgn-2025]

**Coupling [INFERRED]:** keyhole-plus-clasp is effectively the consequence of a high neck: the
keyhole is the opening that lets the head through, so a tool should create it automatically when a
high, mock or halter-high neck is chosen.[^styles] See [constraints](/domain/garment/choice-constraints.md).

**Gaps:** no source gives clasp sizes other than Quatro's 20 mm, zip lengths, or how clasps are
attached; see [/domain/construction/](/domain/construction/about.md).
