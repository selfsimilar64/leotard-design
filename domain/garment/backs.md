# Takeaway

Back style is the most varied structural choice and the one brands name most carefully. Families:
closed, scoop (classic, deep, low), V, keyhole (small, standard, large, circular; with or without
straps or binding), clasp-closed high back, strappy (parallel, criss-cross, multi-strap, ladder,
laced, twisted), open, racerback, mesh-filled or double-layer, and asymmetric.[^styles] **Training
lines default to scoop backs; competition lines to keyhole/clasp or strappy backs.** No storefront
reviewed offers "back style" as a filter; the names live in titles, feature bullets and custom
menus.[^styles] USA Gymnastics bans backless and racerback leotards in Development/Xcel
competition.[^usag-rp]

# What shapes back design

- **USA Gymnastics (2026-27):** backless and racerback leotards are inappropriate. "Backless" = the
  open area visually extends more than about 2 inches below the bottom of the shoulder blade, or
  wider than the vertical midpoint of the shoulder blade, unless filled with mesh or flesh-coloured
  fabric. 0.20 deduction after a warning.[^usag-rp] See
  [USA Gymnastics rules](/domain/rules/usa-gymnastics-attire-rules.md).
- **FIG:** back neckline no lower than the lower line of the shoulder blades. See
  [FIG rules](/domain/rules/fig-attire-rules.md).
- GK labels every special-order back as compliant with FIG and USA Gymnastics rules.[^gk-9661s]
- **Spotting hazard:** Snowflake warns very strappy backs can catch a coach's hand while spotting.[^snowflake-training]
- **Embellishment room:** GK notes a plain scoop back leaves the most room for embellishment.[^gk-4959s]
- **High neck → keyhole + clasp:** see [closures](/domain/garment/closures.md) and
  [constraints](/domain/garment/choice-constraints.md).

NCAA reviewer opinion (2025-2026): strappy backs praised (Air Force, Iowa State, Michigan); open
backs (Brown, Air Force); a wish for low backs to return (Utah, Minnesota); a call to retire lace-up
straps and threading or faux threading; a delicate neck clasp praised (Arizona); mesh back with
player names (Bridgeport) and jersey-style names on the back (Utah).[^cgn-2025][^cgn-2026]

# Suggested model [INFERRED]

`base opening` (closed, scoop + depth, V + depth, keyhole + size/shape, fully open) + `upper
closure` (none, back-neck clasp, invisible zip) + `strap set` ([straps](/domain/garment/straps.md))
+ `fill` (none, mesh, nude-lined mesh, printed faux straps) + `symmetry`. Default suggestions differ
by purpose (training: scoop; competition: keyhole/clasp or strappy).[^styles]

# Gaps

Not found as brand terms: butterfly back, cage back, teardrop keyhole, double keyhole, X-back,
Y-back, deep U. `T-Back` is undefined. No source gives numeric keyhole sizes or back-scoop depths.
Alpha Factor, Ozone and Christian Moreau menus could not be read.[^styles]
