# Takeaway

Sleeves vary on five independent axes: **length**, **fabric** (opaque body fabric, unlined mesh,
or sublimated/ombré mesh), **cap construction** (raglan is named; set-in is implied), **symmetry**
(matched, an "opposite sleeve" in a different fabric, or one sleeve / one shoulder), and **surface
treatment** (cuff lettering, outer-arm stripes, crystal patterns). Mesh sleeves are now the default
at the top level.[^styles][^cornell] Never run a straight high-contrast stripe along the inner arm:
on bars it advertises any bend in the arm.[^cornell]

# Length

Labels: sleeveless/tank; `cap sleeve`; `Short Sleeve`; three-quarter (`3Q Sleeve`, `3/4 Sleeve`,
`3 Quarter Length Sleeve`); long (`Full Sleeve`, `Long Sleeve`, `LS`).[^sylviap-cat][^quatro-lite][^gk-special]
Frequencies are in [garment types](/domain/garment/garment-types-and-silhouettes.md). NCAA allows
only long or sleeveless (Rule 1.10.c, 2026-27).[^ncaa-rules]

Sleeve length changes crystal count: Sylvia P heavy-crystal Esmerelda is over 2,220 stones long
sleeve versus over 1,880 three-quarter; Beverly over 2,910 versus over 2,520 (about 340-390 fewer
stones going from full to three-quarter).[^sylviap-cat]

# Fabric

- GK design director Jeanne Diaz: mesh has become standard in sleeves and upper bodices, driven by
  athlete requests.[^cornell]
- GK vocabulary: `mesh sleeves`, `black mesh sleeves`, `sublimated mesh sleeves`,
  `mesh sleeves and shoulders`, mesh sleeves with a light crystal scatter, sublimated ombré sleeves
  on `SubFuse` fabric, white `Mesh` sleeves sublimated with a colour gradient.[^gk-special][^gk-9661s][^gk-4959s]
- Lining convention: sleeves are **unlined**. Quatro pairs a nude-lined upper bodice with unlined,
  breathable sublimated mesh sleeves; Sylvia P lines all mesh *body* panels and lists sleeves
  separately.[^quatro-ls][^sylviap-cat]
- 11 of 405 Quatro descriptions mention mesh sleeves.[^quatro-all]

# Cap construction

Quatro names raglan: `3.4 raglan mesh sleeves` (Treille) and raglan-style sleeves credited with
better mobility (Vortex); 5 of 405 Quatro descriptions mention raglan.[^quatro-all] "Set-in" was not
found on any brand page; it is assumed as the default where raglan is not stated.[^styles]

# Symmetry

- Four of Sylvia P's 20 2026-27 designs (Ruby, Fern, Selene, Trixie) have one sleeve in body fabric
  and an `Opposite sleeve` in mesh.[^sylviap-cat]
- NCAA: a trend toward one sleeve in a contrasting colour (2025); Auburn's one-shoulder `Bloom` and
  Michigan State's `Lambda` with contrasting sleeves (2026); Alabama's 2009 single mesh sleeve cited
  as a 2000s hallmark.[^cgn-2025][^cgn-2026][^ncaa-com-2024]
- 13 of 405 Quatro descriptions mention asymmetric panels or sleeves.[^quatro-all]

# Surface treatment

- **Cuffs carry branding:** in 2026 NCAA teams put the team name on cuffs as graphic print (Kent
  State, Clemson) or rhinestones (UCLA, Missouri).[^cgn-2026]
- **Stripes:** an emerging 2027 element (Towson colour racing stripe, Utah State rhinestone stripe,
  Oklahoma's tapered solid white panel over mesh sleeves); the US `Luminous Legacy` Olympic leotard
  had crystal stripes along the sleeves.[^cgn-2026][^nbc-2024]
- **Crystals and lettering:** GK offers team names on either sleeve in crystals (#4935S) and
  `Brilliance standard crystals on sleeves` on stock styles.[^gk-special][^gk-stock]
- **Wrap motifs:** Snowflake's Egypt Olympic leotard had a crystal snake twisting round the right
  arm.[^insidegym-2024]
- Sylvia P includes `SP Hydro-Protect` as standard in full and three-quarter sleeved competitive
  leotards; what it physically is was not found.[^sylviap-design]

# Suggested parameters [INFERRED]

`length` (none, cap, short, three-quarter, full) · `cap construction` (set-in, raglan) · `fabric`
(body, unlined mesh, sublimated mesh with gradient) · `symmetry` (matched, opposite-sleeve swap,
single sleeve / one shoulder) · `cuff` (plain hem, contrast band, lettered band) · `overlay`
(outer-arm stripe, tapered opaque panel over mesh, wrap motif) · `crystal zone`. Crystal budget
should recompute when sleeve length changes.[^styles]

# Gaps

No source on cuff construction (elastic, binding width, finger loops); no bell, flutter or
detachable sleeves seen in any 2026 WAG catalogue.[^styles]
