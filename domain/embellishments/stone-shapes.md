# Takeaway

Non-round flat-backs exist in many families but **only a few fixed sizes each**, and leotard
makers offer a short menu: GK sells "shaped jewels";[^gk-distinguished] Sylvia P offers navette,
square, kite and pear;[^sylviap-emb] Quatro round, boat and waterdrop;[^quatro-stones] GK groups its
Swarovski range into seven shapes.[^gk-blog-emb][^report] The app's shape library can therefore be
a small fixed catalogue rather than a parametric shape tool. **[INFERRED]**

# Application note

Preciosa notes some "fancy stones" can only be hot-fixed with heat from the back of the fabric.[^preciosa-manual]
Shaped stones are larger than the machine range (2–5 mm), so treat them as hand-set accents; see
[placement rules](/domain/embellishments/stone-placement-rules.md). **[INFERRED]**

No manufacturer list of **hotfix** shaped stones (Swarovski hotfix catalogue, Preciosa fancy-stone
catalogue) was retrieved.[^ecs]
