# Takeaway

Industrial practice: a **CAD program fixes every stone's position**, a **setting machine** places
stones face-down onto heat-transfer film, a **heat press** bonds the motif to the **still-flat cut
pieces**, larger stones and pearls go on **by hand with tweezers**, and only then are the pieces
sewn together.[^foley][^nam][^nbcphl][^report] Press at roughly 160–170 °C for 15–25 s at medium
pressure with stretch fabric held slightly stretched; cool 5 minutes; cure 24 hours.[^preciosa-manual]
Exact recipes: [hotfix press parameters](/domain/embellishments/hotfix-press-parameters.md).

# Steps (GK, from press reporting)

1. CAD layout: "With such a high number of crystals, a computer-aided design program is needed to
   determine exact crystal placement".[^foley]
2. Machine setting onto transfer film: a rhinestone-transfer machine places crystals on
   heat-transfer paper; a "precision laser with a presser foot" applies crystals individually;
   "Jewel machines create a crystal embellishment … on a flat sheet".[^nam][^foley][^nbcphl] Rio 2016
   stones were set "with the help of a machine with robot arms";[^sportslogos] in 2008 (184 crystals)
   they were mostly hand-placed.[^jewelryjudge]
3. Heat press: "The embellishment is then sealed onto the leotard using a heat press"; "fill-in
   jewels by laser machine", some by hand.[^nbcphl]
4. Hand finishing: for 2024 GK "applied larger crystals by hand using tweezers"; pearls hand-placed.[^foley][^ecs]
5. Seaming after embellishment (panels sewn together after crystal application).[^nam][^report]
   Consequence for design: layouts crossing side, shoulder or crotch seams need splitting or a
   hand-applied finishing pass (see [placement rules](/domain/embellishments/stone-placement-rules.md)
   and [/domain/construction/](/domain/construction/about.md)).

Crystal usage "has exploded over the past two decades, thanks to improving technology and machinery".[^nam]

single-head setting (excluding loading and pressing); at 600/min ≈ 11 and 17 min.[^ecs]

**[INFERRED]** Production hand-off should assume the Sierra/CAMS ecosystem: one layer per hopper, up
to six size/colour combinations per pass, 2–5 mm stones, 400 × 400 mm field, plus vector export for
cutters.[^ecs]

# Template (non-machine) method

Cut a template (flock, 0.5–1 mm card, or acrylic), pour stones on and brush so they seat face-up,
lift with heat-resistant transfer tape, press, cool, peel. With mixed sizes fill big stones first or
cut one template per size/colour.[^edutech-hotfix][^ru-cricut] Hole sizing: Stitch Era's default
cutting margin adds ≈0.3 mm to the stone radius (0.2 mm for laser); leave ≥1 mm between stones;[^edutech-stitchera]
Inkscape practice adds "about 1 mm" to the stone.[^edutech-inkstitch] **[UNVERIFIED]** template
circles 0.100 in (ss6), 0.135 in (ss10), 0.176 in (ss16).[^trw-korean]

# Hand methods (repairs, large stones, one-offs)

- Non-hotfix stones glued one by one with gem glue (Gem-Tac, "dries completely clear", fine-tip
  syringe; E6000 also named); wax-tipped pick-up tool; garment on a torso-shaped "Dolly Board", "flat
  but not over stretched".[^cp-stretch][^cp-guide]
- Glue "formulated to stay flexible after curing"; avoid super glue, acetone glues and hot glue.
  Correctly applied hotfix glue is "one of the strongest fabric adhesives available".[^ru-washing]
- Heated wand and iron settings: see [press parameters](/domain/embellishments/hotfix-press-parameters.md).
- Stone loss in use is "not unusual" on stretch garments.[^cp-stretch]

# Care

Hotfixed textiles "can be washed at 60 °C, tumble-dried, dry-cleaned and carefully ironed"; coated
stones 30 °C gentle, inside out, in a wash bag.[^preciosa-manual] GK leotard care: hand wash cold,
no soaking, hang dry, no iron; softeners break down adhesives (foil discolours, embellishments fall
off); hairspray, perfume, lotion, deodorant damage foil.[^gk-care]

# Lead times (time-sensitive)

GK Special Order 5–6 weeks, Stock-with-Embellishment 3–4 weeks;[^gk-blog-2026] a high-profile custom
leotard 6–8 weeks; Olympic collections about two years.[^nbcphl]

Gaps: hand-stoning labour rates, machine file formats, hotfix durability under repeated stretch on
nylon/spandex (a Czech thesis at dspace.tul.cz could not be fetched).[^ecs]
