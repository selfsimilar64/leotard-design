# Takeaway

What gymnastics makers call spangles are **hole-less plastic discs punched from reels** by a
spangle-transfer machine onto transfer tape and heat-pressed — "no threads or holes" — the flat,
light, cheap cousin of the rhinestone transfer.[^coldesi-spangle] Sizes 2–5 mm (up to 6 mm on a
four-head machine) in holographic, metallic, matte and glossy finishes.[^coldesi-prospangle][^coldesi-machines]
Trade equivalence: a 5 mm spangle has the "visual impact" of an ss20 rhinestone.[^coldesi-prospangle]
Sewn sequins are essentially a dance/costume technique.[^ecs]

# On leotards

- GK lists only "silver hologram spanglez™" on stock leotards and offers spanglez for team names and
  logos.[^gk-stock][^gk-resilient] **[UNVERIFIED]** (search summary) "silver hologram sequinz",
  "bedazzled with sequinz in a firecracker pattern".[^dicks]
- Sylvia P lists 2–5 mm sequins in iridescent, holographic, glitter and mirror finishes.[^sylviap-emb][^report]
- **[INFERRED]** GK's spanglez/sequinz are almost certainly heat-applied hole-less spangles (GK does
  not define them); they suit lettering, logos and dense fills where weight and cost matter, while
  crystals carry the main design.[^ecs]
- Spangles are marketed for dance/cheer uniforms and kids' wear as light and non-scratching ("soft,
  smooth, and kid-friendly").[^spangleelite][^coldesi-machines]
- GK will not heat-apply spanglez/sequinz to velvet (see [placement rules](/domain/embellishments/stone-placement-rules.md)).[^gk-resilient]

# Sewn sequins (secondary library)

Attached with a single embroidery thread, not the most secure, damaged in the wash, the centre hole
shows fabric.[^coldesi-spangle] Punched from sheet; some multi-faceted for reflectivity or stamped
with lobes; stitched flat (stable) or at one point so they dangle and catch more light; materials
moved from metal to gelatin (1930s) to clear plastic, polyester film and vinyl.[^wiki-sequin]

# Model for the app

**[INFERRED]** A spangle is a flat disc (or simple die shape) lying in the fabric surface with one
planar reflective face. Parameters: diameter (2/3/4/5/6 mm), finish (holographic, metallic, matte,
glossy), colour. It behaves like a single mirror facet following the fabric normal.[^ecs]
