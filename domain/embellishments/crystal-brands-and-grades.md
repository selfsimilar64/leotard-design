# Takeaway

Premium flat-backs in 2026 are **Preciosa Chaton Rose MAXIMA** (Czech, lead-free, 15 facets in
common sizes) and **Swarovski XIRIUS** (2078 hotfix / 2088), with GK still using Swarovski for the
2024 Olympic leotards.[^ecs][^foley] Below sit 12-facet stones (Preciosa VIVA12, LUX, DMC) and 8–11-facet
Korean/Chinese glass used for bulk machine-set transfers.[^ecs] Availability facts here are
time-sensitive (`stale_after` set).

# Swarovski availability (time-sensitive)

- 2020: Swarovski said it would "withdraw from offering Swarovski® components that are tailored for
  the DIY and nails retail segment" in 2021, exiting "low-margin wholesale"; new components for
  dance, costuming, DIY and nail customers stopped in November 2021. CEO Robert Buchbauer: "…less is
  more."[^ru-restructure] April 2020: 750 stores closed, about 6,000 layoffs; Alexis Nasard CEO from 2022.[^wiki-swarovski]
- **[UNVERIFIED]** (search summary only) From 2021 buyers signed a Brand Control Agreement; one
  distributor's criteria: no use of the Swarovski name in marketing, at least US$2,000/year, no
  resale of loose stones.[^firemountain]
- **[UNVERIFIED]** **single-source**: effective 1 June 2026 the Brand Control Agreement is no longer
  required; authorised channels may sell to independent designers, small businesses, dance studios
  etc.; the retailer stocks 2038, 2058, 2078, 2088 in Crystal and Crystal AB and special-orders the
  rest. No Swarovski statement was found.[^ru-2026]
- Supply still thin in October 2026: "minimal inventory in the U.S. … plan for 2-3 weeks" for more
  than 1–2 packages.[^ru-2078-16]

# Swarovski cuts

- 2088 XIRIUS Rose: "multilayer cut technology"; sizes ss12–ss48. 2058 XILION Rose Enhanced ss5–10;
  2000 Rose ss3–4. No-hotfix backs carry "platinum foiling".[^swarovski-2018]
- 2078 = hotfix counterpart of 2088;[^ru-2078-16] 16 cuts (8 large + 8 small) plus table.[^edutech-hotfix]
- XILION introduced 2004 for Roses and Chatons; all Swarovski crystal lead-free since 2012.[^wiki-swarovski]
- Pending patents: gemstone-cutting method EP4371443A1 (filed 2022); coloured coating applied as ink
  in a polymerisable carrier via plasma, US 2023/0272234 A1 (filed 2021).[^foley]

# Preciosa

- Two round lines, both hotfix and non-hotfix: **VIVA12** (12 facets, ss5/1.8 mm–ss48/11 mm,
  "silver/grey" foil) and **MAXIMA** (ss5–ss20 15 facets, ss30–ss48 18 facets, "light silver" foil);
  colours match across lines.[^cp-viva-maxima]
- MAXIMA retailer-relayed claims: 15 facets in most-used sizes, 18 large, 12 micro; "reflects up
  to 264 unique rays of light"; "100+ colors and coatings"; lead-free; "higher refractive index"
  (no value).[^ru-preciosa-leader] **Conflict**: the same retailer's 30ss page says "15-facet".[^ru-maxima-30]
- Preciosa's benchmark sheet: MAXIMA 12–18 facets, ss2–ss48 (smallest hotfix ss3, 12 facets);
  Swarovski Xirius/Xilion/Xero 8–16; Stellux 12 facets ss6–ss40; about 70 colours and 90
  coatings/effects for MAXIMA.[^preciosa-benchmark]
- Manual name "MC Chaton Rose MAXIMA Hotfix"; the group "melts 40 tons of glass every day".[^preciosa-manual]
- **[INFERRED]** "264 rays" is marketing with no method; do not use as a physical parameter.[^ecs]
