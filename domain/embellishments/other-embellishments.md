# Takeaway

Modern WAG leotards also use metal **nailheads/rhinestuds**, **pearls** (hand-placed accents),
**laser-cut appliqué** glued then stitched, **embroidered** logos, velvet and mesh insets, and
printed/foil fabrics (sublimation, Mystique foil-dot, hologram). These are surface layers the stones
sit on and must be compatible with.[^ecs] Fabric details: [/domain/fabrics/](/domain/fabrics/about.md).

# Nailheads and rhinestuds

- GK offers "crystals/nailheads".[^gk-stock]
- Rhinestuds: aluminium, facet-shaped "to emulate rhinestones", lighter, lead-free; "four sizes",
  "dozens of colors"; 3 mm in 500-gross bags (72,000 pieces, US$100.24). High dryer heat "can loosen
  the bond".[^coldesi-rstud]
- **[UNVERIFIED]** 3, 4, 5 mm in twenty colours, "a flat surface similar to nail heads";[^stahls]
  "essentially colored nailheads that are faceted like crystals".[^dharma]
- The same machines set rhinestones, rhinestuds and nailheads.[^coldesi-cams]
- **[INFERRED]** Render as small metal domes or faceted metal caps: opaque coloured-metal
  reflectance, no transparency or fire, lower than a same-diameter crystal.[^ecs]

# Pearls

- Swarovski pearls appeared on USA Gymnastics leotards for the first time at Paris 2024; "Freedom's
  Grace" has almost 1,000 hand-placed pearls.[^usag-2024][^people]
- Hand applicator kits include a "Hot Spot" tip for pearls and shaped stones.[^ru-methods]
- **[INFERRED]** Hotfix pearls are half-domes with a soft broad pearlescent highlight (the visual
  opposite of a crystal glint); treat as individually placed accents, not fill elements.[^ecs]

# Appliqué and embroidery

- "Laser cut appliques are then hand-placed and glued to the body of the leotard" and then stitched;
  one Tokyo-cycle leotard carried 76 appliqué stars.[^nbcphl]
- Embroidery is used for federation and maker logos.[^nam]

# Print and fabric surfaces

- Sublimation: printed on paper and heat-transferred at about 450 °F; on "Star Spangled Shine" jewels
  and sublimation carve negative space over mesh with small dots.[^nam][^nbcdfw]
- Mystique (nylon/spandex with small foil dots) used since 2004; 2024 leotards added four-way-stretch
  satin, custom red stretch satin, velvet accents, and more matte fabrics — "they like matte fabrics
  with crystals. Shiny fabrics are becoming less of a go-to choice".[^foley][^marieclaire][^nbcdfw]
- 2026 NCAA non-crystal examples: "beaded neckline" (Auburn), "glittery 'RIC' lettering", full velvet
  bodies with contrasting rhinestones (Illinois), team names on cuffs in print vs rhinestones.[^cgn-2026-02][^cgn-2026-05]
- Glitter heat-transfer vinyl (general, not leotard-specific): Siser Glitter 12 in wide, at least 41
  colours.[^siser]
- Preciosa sells hotfix "Crystal Sheet" (pressed at 145–165 °C) and ready-made hotfix transfers.[^preciosa-manual]
- **[INFERRED]** Foil-dot and hologram fabrics dull under pressed stones (GK's warning), so a renderer
  should darken/flatten foil within the heat-pressed footprint.[^ecs]
