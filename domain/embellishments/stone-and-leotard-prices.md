# Takeaway

Loose **premium stones cost about 6 US cents each at ss16** and 16–22 cents at ss30; **Korean/DMC
glass at ss6–ss10 is well under 1 cent** — roughly an order of magnitude apart (sizes compared
differ, so indicative only).[^ecs] US retail, October 2026 unless noted; re-check before use.
