Trade facts about cutting, sewing, decorating and finishing leotards, independent of our software,
distilled from the patternmaking research note.[^pc-notes] This is the knowledge the professional
tool needs so that a design drawn on the body arrives at a [seamstress](/product/personas/seamstress.md)
or factory as flat pattern pieces they can cut and sew.

**The one-paragraph version.** Factories cut, print and decorate flat pieces, then close the side,
shoulder and crotch seams last. Pieces are cut smaller than the body (negative ease) by an amount no
manufacturer publishes. Greatest stretch always runs around the body. A design line is either a
pieced seam (6 mm allowances, straight or gentle curves only), an appliqué overlay (any shape) or a
sublimated print (no seam). Openings are finished with about 1 cm elastic or binding. Almost every
manufacturability threshold is unpublished, so every rule here is an adjustable default.
