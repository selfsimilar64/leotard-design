# Takeaway

**Linings are a separate, lighter cloth (85-92 g/m²) in a small set of nude tones plus white, black
and brown; they sit under the outer fabric as front lining, under mesh bodices, and as
gussets.**[^notes-fab] They are semi-opaque (Carvico calls Siviglia "sheer"), so a lining changes the
look of mesh above it. How they are used per panel is in the [fabric stack](/domain/fabrics/fabric-stack.md).

| Article | Composition | g/m² | Stretch | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carvico 204 Siviglia ("ultra-light, sheer, extra-soft ... lining") | 90% PA / 10% EA | 85 +/-10% (140 g per linear m) | **215% width / 120% length** | 165 cm; 37 x 20 per cm; modulus at 40% 20-40 g / 40-80 g; pilling 4, 3/4; wash shrinkage up to -8% in length; Bianco, Nero, Nudo[^carvico-siviglia][^carvico-siviglia-page][^evoli-lining] |
| Spandex World Stretch Lining - Nylon (Nude) | 84% nylon / 16% spandex | 92 | 4-way | $6.00/yd; poly and "Heavy Weight" versions in nude, white, black, blush[^sw-21382][^sw-lining] |
| GK Shadowproof Lining | **print catalogue: 94% PES / 6% spandex; web store: 81% nylon / 19% spandex or 78% PES / 22% spandex** | n/p | | White, Black, Brown, Dark Brown, Nude; available in binding and piping[^gk-p128][^gk-swatch] |

All also in the notes.[^notes-fab] Siviglia is, with the Shiro print base, one of the two Carvico
articles that stretch more across the width than along the length (see
[stretch properties](/domain/fabrics/stretch-properties.md)).

Fabric reseller advice (commercial, untested): use a double layer of lining with crossed grain under
heavy crystal work.[^evoli-guide]

**Contradiction to resolve:** GK's own two sources disagree on Shadowproof's fibre and blend; ask GK
or trust neither for composition-driven logic.[^notes-fab]
