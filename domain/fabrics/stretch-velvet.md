# Takeaway

**Velvet is the one family whose look is dominated by its pile: "a dense pile of cut fibers that
form a smooth nap", so shade changes with the direction of light and view.**[^lineup-novelty] It has
almost disappeared from GK's competition range (two colours, 016 Black and 165 Sapphire, in
2026-27).[^gk-p129][^notes-fab] Smooth velvet is 85% PA / 15% EA (Carvico) or 86-90% PES / 10-14% EA,
210-312 g/m², 2-way or 4-way, opaque; crushed, burnout, foiled and sequinned velvets are 85-95% PES or
PA with 5-15% EA, 176-320 g/m², mostly 2-way, opaque to semi-sheer.[^notes-fab] Uses: bodies (now
rare), inserts, binding, collars.[^notes-fab]

# Rules and warnings

- No heat-applied Spanglez and no imprinting on velvet (GK).[^gk-p129] See
  [decoration compatibility](/domain/fabrics/rule-decoration-and-trim-compatibility.md).
- GK names Rich Red Velvet among higher colour-bleed risks.[^gk-care]
- Cut every velvet piece with the pile running the same way (fabric reseller advice, commercial and
  untested).[^evoli-guide] **[INFERRED]** Nap direction is therefore a per-panel property the app
  should store.
- **Gap:** no velvet pile height was found.[^notes-fab]
