# Takeaway

**The failure modes makers warn about are foil dulling and loss, colour bleed between dark and light
panels, pilling of printed polyester and matte nylon on rough surfaces, and snagging or tearing of
mesh.**[^notes-fab] Matte nylon tricot is the most resilient. **[INFERRED]** A per-fabric durability
attribute could rank: matte nylon tricot (best) > shiny nylon and polyester tricot (pilling risk when
printed) > velvet > mystique and hologram foil > mesh, foil mesh, lace and glitter (most
fragile).[^notes-fab]

# What makers say

- Sylvia P: Matt Lycra most resilient, least fading; Mystique and Diamond Mesh sensitive; Mesh and Fog
  Mesh delicate, prone to pulling or tearing; all fabrics carry a "risk of colours fading, bleeding or
  pilling"; avoid rough surfaces and Velcro (Velcro, concrete, gym-mat friction), especially on the
  bottom; will not replace pilled garments.[^sp-fabric][^sp-care]
- GK: Hologram, Mesh and Mystique are fragile; close athlete contact can damage mesh; mesh lifespan not
  guaranteed; deodorant and sweat cause bleed, fading or foil loss, especially under the arms (recommends
  aluminium-free crystal deodorant); colour transfer from a dark warm-up or damp bag cannot be fully
  prevented by washing; leotards should not shrink but may stretch with wear.[^gk-care]
- Plum: mesh may be damaged by person-to-person contact; "Machine drying with heat and tumbling will
  cause embellishments to fall off."[^plum-faq]
- Mill pilling grades (Martindale, 7000 rotations, 5 best; fabric / wool): matte and shiny recycled-nylon
  tricot 5/5; polyester print bases 5/4-5; Beverly satin 4-5/4; velvet 4-5/3-4; mesh 4-5/4-5; lining
  4/3-4 (see [stretch properties](/domain/fabrics/stretch-properties.md)).[^notes-fab]
- Base tricots carry swim-grade claims: chlorine, sun-cream and oil resistance (Vita); UPF 50+
  (Milliskin).[^carvico-vita-page][^sw-796]
- The 2012 US Olympic leotards were athlete wear-tested and wash-tested.[^buzzfeed-2012]

# Care instructions conflict

| Maker | Wash | Dry |
|---|---|---|
| GK | Hand wash cold, mild liquid detergent, separately, inside out; no softener, dry cleaning or ironing[^gk-care] | Hang |
| Snowflake | Cold water with 1/4 cup table salt, no soap or detergent, knead gently; never rub or soak[^snowflake-wash] | Roll in towel, air-dry on plastic hanger |
| Plum | Hand wash cold, mild detergent[^plum-faq] | Pat dry, hang; no heat |
| Sylvia P | Machine, inside out, wool wash, short cold cycle; no soak, bleach, softener, ironing, sunlight[^sp-care] | **Tumble dry immediately on low or no heat** |
| Quatro | Cold gentle machine, alone, no bleach, detergent or conditioner; "do not hand-wash"; six-month stone replacement[^quatro-faq] | Remove straight after spin |
| Ozone | "Machine wash individually on a delicate cycle (cold water)."[^ozone-care] | Dry flat; **no tumble drying** |

**[INFERRED]** Common core: cold, alone, inside out, no softener, no soaking, no heat on foil.[^notes-fab]
Chalk is not mentioned on any care page found.[^notes-fab]

**Gaps:** no colourfastness grades (wash, perspiration, rubbing) for any leotard fabric; no abrasion
test to failure for foil; no stated service life in wears or washes.[^notes-fab]
