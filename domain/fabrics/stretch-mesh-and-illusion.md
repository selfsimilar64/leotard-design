# Takeaway

**Stretch mesh (also "stretch net" or "elastic tulle") is the sheer layer of a leotard, and the most
fragile.** Fine nets are 80-87% PA / 13-20% EA (or 83% PES / 17% EA printable) at 65-115 g/m²,
four-way, translucent; used for sleeves, yokes, cut-outs, nude "illusion" panels and as a base for
crystals.[^notes-fab][^dsi-mesh] Denser power mesh (80-88% PA, 150-175 g/m², semi-sheer) is for
support panels and built-in bras.[^notes-fab] GK: "Mesh is a fragile fabric, not a durable fabric."
and "We recommend Mesh only be used as accents within a design."[^gk-swatch]

# Types in trade use

The Lineup: micro-mesh (four-way, 50 colours matched to tricot); Nude Mesh ("Perfect for nude
illusion insets and offered in 5 different skin tones.": Mocha, Sable, Toffee, Chestnut, Cocoa);
Metallic, Glitter, Athletic, Pop Mesh (1/4 in holes) and Big Hole Mesh (1/2 in holes).[^lineup-mesh]
DSI London: stretch mesh's "recovery capacity is remarkable"; Powernet is firmer for bodices.[^dsi-mesh]
Skin-tone nets are sold in named flesh shades (e.g. 702/232 Beige, 0254 Sable/Skin, Flesh, 0253
Mandorla Beige, 30015 Coffee Cream).[^evoli-net] Sylvia P: 4-way, soft but delicate, prone to pulling
or tearing on rough surfaces; 23 colours including Light, Mid and Deep Nude.[^sp-fabric] Plum: mesh may
be damaged by person-to-person contact during routines.[^plum-faq]

# Contradiction

Chrisanne Clover lists Stretch Net at 87% polyamide / 13% elastane and Power Net at 87/13, while a
second Chrisanne page gives stretch net as 94.5% nylon / 5.5% spandex (both via search summary; pages
returned 403).[^chrisanne-body][^chrisanne-net][^notes-fab]

# Gaps

No denier, hole count, hole pitch or open-area percentage was found for any fine stretch net; "power
mesh" and "illusion mesh" are trade descriptions with no common standard.[^notes-fab]
