# Takeaway

**Printed leotards use a separate family of white polyester-elastane tricots, because sublimation
dye only bonds to polyester.** They are 75-87% polyester / 13-25% elastane at 190-250 g/m², matte to
shiny, opaque, supplied white with colour printed in, used for printed bodies, training leotards and
ombré; they are prone to pilling.[^notes-fab] Printable foil and hologram variants exist and are
always polyester (see [mystique](/domain/fabrics/mystique-foil-dot.md)). The process and its limits
are in [sublimation printing](/domain/fabrics/sublimation-printing.md).

# Pilling

GK warns that pilling may occur on Primatex and SubFuse, especially on the back and bottom, from
contact with rough surfaces;[^gk-7659] Sylvia P says sublimated fabrics are prone to pilling and it
is "not liable if pilling occurs".[^notes-fab] Mill pilling grades for polyester print bases are 5
(fabric) and 4/5 (wool), Martindale 7000 rotations.[^carvico-tobago][^notes-fab]
