# Takeaway

**Hologram fabrics are mystique's sibling: the foil is diffractive, so colour changes with the angle
of light and view.** "A tricot treated on the surface with a foil-like hologram, it is great for
shining on the floor."[^lineup-novelty] Composition 80-83% nylon / 17-20% elastane, or 80% polyester /
20% elastane; 200-235 g/m²; opaque; four-way stretch; foil in dots, shards or patterns; used for trim,
accent panels and full bodies.[^notes-fab] GK classes hologram as fragile ("embellished with a
holographic foil that demands gentle care").[^gk-swatch][^gk-care]

# Appearance (qualitative only)

Observed in swatch photos (researcher's own reading): GK "Sparkle" looks like coarse multicoloured
glitter flakes, "Opalescence" smoother and pearlescent; Quatro's "Silver Holo", "Magenta Holo" and
"Diesel" show rainbow or oil-slick hue shifts across a fold.[^notes-fab] No measured grating period,
dot size or colour-flop data exists for any hologram fabric.[^notes-fab]

Care, threats and dulling are as for [mystique](/domain/fabrics/mystique-foil-dot.md); GK adds that
alcohol in hairspray and aerosols damages hologram fabrics.[^gk-care] Rendering:
[holographic diffraction](/engineering/rendering/fabric-shading/holographic-diffraction.md).
