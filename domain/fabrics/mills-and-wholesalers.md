# Takeaway

**Carvico (Italy) is the one mill found publishing true technical data sheets; US converters publish
composition, weight, width and price; which mill supplies GK, Sylvia P or Quatro is not stated
anywhere.**[^notes-fab] Carvico's figures are in [stretch properties](/domain/fabrics/stretch-properties.md).

| Supplier | Role | What they publish / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carvico (Italy) | Mill | 60 articles incl. Aldabra 364 crushed velvet, Argentina 369 very shiny, Aspen 301 and Igloo 304 super-matte PES for transfer printing, Colorado 488 peach-skin micro-PA, Rodi Rec 117, Valencia 220 and Alicante PL 212 PES mesh; full data sheets for 10 articles[^carvico-fabrics] |
| Evoli (EU) | Carvico reseller by the metre | Sells Vita/Malaga as "Matt Lycra", Sumatra "Glossy", Beverly "Shiny", Alicante "Stretch Net", Siviglia lining, Panama and Aldabra velvet; publishes commercial (untested) sewing advice[^evoli-shop] |
| Pine Crest Fabrics (Portland, Oregon stock) | Converter / wholesaler | Foil lines from Korea (Jewels, Sparkly Jewels, Starlet Holo, Kaleidoscope, Printable Jewels, Super Shine, Rainbow Foil Velvet); "Gymnastics" use filter; Portland stock minimum 15 yd; made-to-order "typically 300 – 500 yards"; $500 minimum first purchase; prices by quote; "Most of our fabrics are produced in Asian factories located in Vietnam, Korea, Taiwan, and China"; offers a digital file for 3D software for print bases[^pinecrest-foil][^pinecrest-faq][^pinecrest-printable] |
| Spandex World (US) | Retail by the yard | Composition, GSM, width, stretch and price per product; GSM and oz figures sometimes do not convert (treat GSM as approximate)[^sw-home] |
| Solid Stone Fabrics (US) | Wholesale | 687 products in "Holograms & Foils" (Voltage, Metallic Sheen, Starbright Glitter Foil, Digital Hologram); sells Carvico Vita in 76 yd rolls[^solidstone-shop][^solidstone-vita] |
| Funki Fabrics / Friedmans (UK) | Print bases and printing | More than 36 sublimation bases with gsm and composition; FoilMe custom foiling[^funki-bases] |
| DSI London (UK) | Dance supplier | Italian two-way lycra in 40+ colours; foiled lycras; meshes; velvets; laces; "commission-dyed" to its palette[^dsi-lycra] |
| Blue Moon Fabrics (US) | Wholesale | Spandex, mesh, netting, velvet, lace and lining in many finishes[^bluemoon] |
| Eurojersey (Italy) | Mill | "3D Digital Library" of warp-knit digital twins for CLO and Browzwear, on request; specs need an article code[^eurojersey-3d][^notes-shade] |

All rows also in the notes.[^notes-fab] Carvico's site mentions no 3D library.[^notes-shade]

**[INFERRED]** Because GK, Snowflake's supplier and the UK makers converge on 80/20 compositions and
Pine Crest's foils are 200 GSM, an 80/20, 200 g/m², 56-60 in mystique is a safe library default.[^notes-fab]

**Gaps:** Spandex House, Fabric Wholesale Direct, Blue Moon product pages, Jersey Lomellina (TLS
failure) and Eurojersey specs were not read; Chrisanne Clover blocks automated reading; mill-direct
prices are not public.[^notes-fab]
