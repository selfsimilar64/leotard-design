# How to read

Visual teardown by a Claude Code subagent from the photos alone.[^moodboard] L and R are the wearer's
unless marked viewer-left/right; CF/CB = centre front/back. Stone sizes: tiny ≈ ss6, small ≈ ss10-12,
medium ≈ ss16-20, large ≈ ss30+. Densities are rough stones per 10 cm². Every figure is an estimate
from a photo **[UNVERIFIED]**; *unclear* marks what the photo does not show.

# Part summary

- **Mesh** in 17 of 20 (sleeves, yokes, CF strips, insets, side triangles); black/smoky most common, then
  skin-tone and coloured; one ombre mesh; 35-60% show-through.
- **Sleeves:** 15 of 20 long or 3/4; raglan common.
- **Necklines:** crew and mock lead; sweetheart illusion yokes in 7+; V in 3; racer high-neck in 1.
- **Waist seams:** straight horizontal (Quatro house style) or Λ/V.
- **Stone layouts:** neckline rays, rows on seams and edges (almost all), density gradients (top-down,
  edge fade, along sleeve, diagonal), swirls/scrolls, geometric diamonds, lattices.
- **Materials:** matte, wet look, mystique/foil, solid metallic, hologram, oil-slick, ombre and
  geometric prints; usually 3-4 per garment; dark-on-dark schemes where only specular differs.
- **Logos/lettering** on the L hip in nearly every image.
- **Unique here:** stacked CB keyholes with bridges; off-centre radial panels with piping; foil-dot
  print only; colour by contour depth (~4,000 stones); corset panels under one ombre; stones clipped
  to print geometry; argyle lattice; crossover X bands; scalloped edges with lace-print mesh;
  oil-slick foil with pinwheel burst; crossing appliqué strip lattice.
