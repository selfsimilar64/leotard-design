# Takeaway

Every brand sizes by body measurement, but labels differ: GK uses `C`/`A` letter sizes, **Quatro
names sizes by chest inches**, Sylvia P by child age and adult letter, Snowflake by `Girls`/`Women's`
letter sizes. Only GK's and Sylvia P's practice-wear numbers were captured; most other charts were
in PDFs, tabs or images.[^styles][^fit] Fit also differs: Destira is said to run "long and skinny"
compared with GK.[^chalkbucket-destira]

# Sylvia P

Practice-wear chart (cm):[^sylviap-practice]

| Size | Chest | Waist | Hip | Torso |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Child 8 | 68 | 60 | 68 | 120 |
| Child 10 | 74 | 64 | 74 | 130 |
| Child 12 | 80 | 68 | 80 | 140 |
| Adult XS | 86 | 72 | 90 | 146 |
| Adult S | 92 | 78 | 96 | 152 |

Teamwear retail sizes seen: `Child 4`, `Child 6`, `Child 8`, `Child 10`, `Child 12`, `Adult XS`,
`Adult S`; larger adult sizes not confirmed. The numeric teamwear chart was not captured.[^sylviap-us][^styles]

# Quatro (named by chest inches)

`22" Child Extra Small`, `24" Child Small`, `26" Intermediate`, `28" Child Medium`,
`30" Child Large`, `32" Adult Extra Small`, `34" Adult Small`, `36" Adult Medium`,
`38" Adult Large`, `40" Adult Extra Large`, `42" Adult Extra Extra Large`,
`44" Adult Extra Extra Extra Large` (12 sizes). Torso is again called the most important
measurement; partial measurements are rounded up. Numeric chart not captured.[^quatro-ls][^quatro-size]

# Snowflake

Workout `Girls XX-Small` to `Girls Large` and `Women's XS` to `Women's XL` (plus `Adult Large`);
competition `Girls Small` to `Girls XL` and `Women's XS` to `Women's XL`; biketards `Girls XS` to
`Girls Large`. Official chart not found.[^snowflake-training][^snowflake-comp][^snowflake-biketards][^fit]

# Destira

Smallest sizes: `Toddler 18-24 months`, `Toddler 2T`, `Child XXS (3T)`, `Child XS (5)`,
`Child S (6)`.[^destira] Third-party torso figures **[UNVERIFIED]**: S 42-44 in, M 45-49 in,
L 50-54 in.[^destira-3rdparty]

# Not found

Official charts for Plum, Alpha Factor and Snowflake; Sylvia P teamwear and Quatro numeric charts;
the adult size ceiling at Sylvia P; any adaptive, maternity or custom-measured competition
leotard.[^styles][^fit]
