# Takeaway

Prices run from about US$20-60 for training and budget team basics, through roughly US$100-380 for
made-to-order club competition leotards, up to about US$700 for crystal-heavy team designs, and an
estimated US$1,000-5,000 for an Olympic piece.[^notes-styles] On a single garment, crystal coverage
alone moves the price 3.5-4x.[^sylviap-nylah] A live count-and-cost read-out therefore speaks the
language buyers already pay in.

# Crystal coverage is the price lever

Sylvia P prices one style by crystal tier (AUD):[^sylviap-nylah]

| Nylah full sleeve | Crystals | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Logo only | about 20 | $125.10 |
| Light | 579+ | $210.90 |
| Light, Swarovski | 579+ | $242.90 |
| Heavy | 1,823+ | $371.80 |
| Heavy, Swarovski | 1,823+ | $471.90 |

Viola (three-quarter sleeve) uses different thresholds: light "over 356" at $204.60, heavy "over
1,560" at $355.70.[^sylviap-viola] **Contradiction:** the StudioSP tool exposes three coverage tiers,
but the 2026-27 catalogue describes five coverage levels and the collection filter includes a
"show piece" level.[^sylviap-catalog][^sylviap-studio]

# Discounts and budget fitting

Snowflake discounts 10% (12-23 pieces), 15% (24-35), 20% (36-47), charges nothing for recolouring
and adds 20% for rush.[^snowflake-team] Quatro adjusts designs "to fit your price point"; Snowflake
says embellishments "can also be customized to fit a budget".[^quatro-custom][^snowflake-team]

# Inferences

- **[INFERRED]** GK's special-order prices look like base prices (crystal patterns are optional on
  product pages), so crystal-heavy GK team leotards cost more than US$378.[^notes-styles]
- **[INFERRED]** Price is driven mainly by three things software can count: sleeve length (tank
  versus sleeved roughly triples the median at Sylvia P), crystal brand and coverage, and
  recoloured-catalogue versus bespoke.[^notes-styles] This is the basis for the live cost band in
  the [UX pattern library](/engineering/ux/ux-pattern-library.md) and matters most to the
  [coach persona](/product/personas/coach-and-gym-owner.md).
- **[INFERRED]** A heavy club design is about 1,500-2,000 stones by Sylvia P's own tiers, which sets
  the scale the editor must handle comfortably.[^notes-tools] See [/domain/embellishments/](/domain/embellishments/about.md).
