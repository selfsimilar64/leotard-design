# Takeaway

Custom leotards are still ordered through a **human loop**: enquiry, designer or sales-rep
consultation, emailed concepts, revisions, sign-off, sizing kits, then **4 to 13 weeks** of
production, usually for teams only, with minimums, and with goods that cannot be returned.[^notes-tools]
Even the self-serve studios end by handing the design to a person.[^notes-tools]

# Steps as published by makers

1. **Enquiry / brief.** Email or phone; no finished design or numbers needed; for replicating a
   kit, send a photo for fabric and colour matching.[^gym-express][^snowflake] Velocity asks about
   the club, colours and gymnast numbers.[^velocity]
2. **Consultation.** A dedicated sales rep (Quatro) or designers working from the customer's
   vision (Snowflake); Varsity's rep runs the design session.[^quatro-process][^snowflake][^varsity]
3. **Design concepts and revisions.** Free at Quatro and Rebel; digital designs "created and refined
   until approved" (Velocity); sketches and fabric sample cards (GK reseller).[^quatro-process][^rebel][^velocity][^gym-express]
   A StudioSP design is forwarded to a state account manager.[^sylviap-nylah]
4. **Sign-off.** Quatro's "secure digital sign-off document"; Rebel's Customer Portal; Varsity
   manufactures and approves a prototype (snippet).[^quatro-process][^rebel][^flocheer]
5. **Sizing.** Physical Fit Kits sent to the gym (GK reseller), sizers so parents choose sizes
   (Snowflake), sample sizes (Velocity), reps who "personally size your athletes" (Varsity), or
   Rebel's "Fit Freedom" sizing without fit kits.[^gym-express][^snowflake][^velocity][^varsity][^rebel]
6. **Production and delivery.** Snowflake runs a free team shop collecting orders and payments;
   Velocity ships direct to members.[^snowflake][^velocity]

# Pain points voiced or implied

- Coaches on a forum say custom orders take "a couple months" and "most companies need at least
  8-10 weeks" (snippets; forum blocked).[^chalkbucket-1][^chalkbucket-2]
- Children grow, "so you don't want to order them too early" (snippet).[^chalkbucket-2] Quatro urges
  clubs to "Plan ahead and order early".[^quatro-timing]
- Rebel markets against the fear that the product won't match the approved picture: artwork
  "translates seamlessly to the final bulk-produced design".[^rebel]
- Makers fit designs to budget after the fact (Quatro "to fit your price point"; Snowflake).[^notes-tools]

These rest on two forum snippets and makers' own messaging, the weakest-evidenced part of the
research. Pain-point research is [deferred](/decisions/2026-10-13-defer-competition-rules-and-pain-points.md).

# Inferences

- **[INFERRED]** Unmet needs: the club's idea is filtered through emails and revision rounds;
  long lead times plus growth compress the decision window; budget is seen after the design, not
  during it; preview fidelity is a trust issue because custom goods cannot be returned;
  individuals and small groups are locked out by team-only rules and minimums.[^notes-tools]
- **[INFERRED]** A self-serve tool does not remove the manufacturer. Its realistic near-term role
  is producing a design the manufacturer can quote and make: panel map, fabric list, stone count by
  size and colour, placement artwork.[^notes-tools] That is the
  [seamstress view](/product/personas/seamstress.md) output and the coach's cost read-out.
