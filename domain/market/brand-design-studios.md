# Takeaway

Three gymnastics brands offer self-serve design studios, and all three are **template-plus-zones
configurators**: pick an existing style, swap fabric and colour per predefined zone, choose a
crystal option, then hand the design to a human rep or account manager.[^notes-tools] No evidence
was found of any brand tool that lets a user draw a new panel line, move a seam or place an
individual stone.[^notes-tools][^report] **All descriptions come from vendor page text; none of
the studios was operated hands-on** (they are interactive WebGL tools the research session could
not drive).[^notes-tools]

# The three studios

| | GK Elite UDesign | Sylvia P StudioSP | Quatro design studio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vendor's 3D claim | "online 3D design studio"; page will not load without WebGL[^gk-udesign] | "our 3D product designer"[^sylviap-nylah] | Not stated[^quatro-studio] |
| Since | At least May 2021, as "UDesign360", launched with Simone Biles's Legacy Collection[^mann-2021] | Not stated | Not stated |
| What changes | "colors, cuts, fabrics, and embellishments"; start from a Special Order style or from scratch[^gk-udesign][^gk-7620] | Per-zone fabric and colour; e.g. zones Upper Panels & Sleeves, Mid Body, Front Insert, Lower Body, Binding & Back Straps, Embellishments; printed bodies up to three colours[^sylviap-nylah] | "Choose your colours, fabrics, and stones"; designs adjusted to price point[^quatro-studio] |
| Crystals | "embellishments" selectable; names and logos in crystals, spanglez or embroidery "on select locations" (snippet)[^gk-7633] | Stone type (clear, AB, coloured; standard or Swarovski) and coverage tier (logo only / light / heavy), **not** placement[^sylviap-nylah] | Swarovski, DMC or standard; round, boat, waterdrop; SS6-SS20; 21 colours[^quatro-stones] |
| Output / hand-off | Not stated; questions go to a local sales rep[^gk-udesign] | "Copy Link" share; "Send to Account Manager" in your state; "Add to bag"[^sylviap-nylah] | Concepts and digital sign-off via sales rep[^quatro-studio] |
| Eligibility | Team purchases only; individuals told to embellish stock leotards[^gk-udesign] | No minimum stated; "Add to bag" suggests individuals can buy[^notes-tools] | Teams and clubs only, 10+ pieces[^quatro-studio] |
| Lead time | "typically 4-6 weeks"[^gk-udesign] | Banner on 2026-10-13: estimated completion 14 Jan 2027[^sylviap-studio] | Custom boutique items "shipped within 12 weeks"[^notes-tools] |

Sylvia P states "Colors are designed to be interchangeable to suit individual organizations
requirements."[^sylviap-clara] A search snippet attributes to Sylvia P a disclaimer that the
garment "may vary from this 3D representation", including "the number and size of crystals";
the text was not found on the pages opened.[^sylviap-sibella] **[UNVERIFIED]**

Even with UDesign, GK's reseller process still uses design sketches, fabric sample cards and
physical "Fit Kits" sent to the gym.[^gym-express] Quatro keeps a human route alongside its studio:
free concepts from its design team, "100% custom leotards. 0% design fees."[^quatro-studio]

# Tools that exist but were not examined

- **Destira**: "READY.SET.CUSTOM." and "Design Your Own" pages exist; body content unreadable.[^notes-tools]
- **Ozone**: an online "Design Studio" page exists; it rendered nothing without accepting a cookie
  banner, which was not accepted.[^ozone][^notes-fabrics]
- **Velocity Pro Sport**: the report mentions a complexity-priced kit builder; the notes record
  Velocity as designer-led (see [brand landscape](/domain/market/brand-landscape.md)).[^report]

# Inferences

- **[INFERRED]** "Real 3D?" GK's WebGL requirement is consistent with a real-time 3D viewer, but
  rendering quality, camera freedom and whether crystals are geometry or texture are unknown.[^notes-tools]
- **[INFERRED]** These studios compete on convenience and ordering integration, not creative
  freedom. They are the closest existing precedent for the
  [coach view](/product/personas/coach-and-gym-owner.md): pick a base style, recolour zones,
  choose a stone option, hand off. What they lack is what the
  [designer view](/product/personas/professional-designer.md) adds.
- **[INFERRED]** Every studio ends at a human (rep, account manager). A new tool's realistic role is
  to produce a design a manufacturer can quote and make; see
  [custom order process](/domain/market/custom-order-process.md).
- Quatro's "kit builder app" wording matches the UK vendor Kit Builder
  ([teamwear configurators](/domain/market/teamwear-configurators.md)), but no source confirms
  Quatro uses it.[^notes-tools] **[UNVERIFIED]**

# Gaps

Per-zone option lists, output formats, pricing and technology of UDesign; hands-on walkthroughs
or videos of any studio. Closing step proposed by the report: an hour hands-on with each.[^report]
