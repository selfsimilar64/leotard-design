# Takeaway

The demand evidence is **supply-side and adjacent-market**. It supports "makers believe customers
want to design for themselves", not "coaches or parents are asking for a 3D tool".[^notes-tools]
No first-hand coach, club-owner, gym-owner or parent statements could be read.[^notes-tools][^report]

# Evidence

| Signal | Strength |
|---|---|
| Three leading brands built self-serve studios: GK (since 2021), Sylvia P (StudioSP), Quatro (design "at a time that suits you")[^mann-2021][^sylviap-nylah][^quatro-studio] | Supply-side: makers invested |
| Simone Biles framed GK's customiser as inviting "the gymnastics community into the process"[^mann-2021] | Marketing |
| Dressmaker reached 35,650 concurrent Steam players within a week of launch[^dlcompare] | Adjacent: consumer appetite for hands-on garment making |
| Sylvia P's crystal tiers move one garment from AU$125 to AU$472, i.e. buyers already choose and pay for decoration[^sylviap-nylah] | Indirect |
| Hobbyist leotard assets and flat templates exist ([unoccupied gap](/domain/market/unoccupied-gap.md)) | Weak, latent |
| Two forum snippets on slow custom orders ([custom order process](/domain/market/custom-order-process.md)) | Weak; forum blocked |
