# Takeaway

GK Elite (US) is the market leader and Team USA's outfitter; Sylvia P (Australia) and Quatro (UK)
are the main international custom teamwear houses and, with GK, the only brands found with
self-serve design studios; Snowflake, Plum and Destira serve US clubs at lower prices; everyone
else routes custom design through a human designer or sales rep.[^notes-styles][^notes-tools]
The UK's Milano Pro Sport ceased trading in July 2026.[^milano] No market-share or revenue figures
exist in the research.[^notes-styles]

# Adjacent cheer and dance brands

Varsity Spirit runs rep-led "Design Studio" sessions, not a self-serve tool, with 35+ colours and 15
fabrics.[^varsity] Rebel Athletic offers free artwork, free revisions, a free physical sample and a
Customer Portal for approving artwork.[^rebel] Omni Cheer has a bundle "package builder", not a
design tool; Yumiko and Elevé (dance) have made-to-order "pick style, pick colours" flows whose
mechanics were not retrieved.[^notes-tools]

# Notes and contradictions

- **Velocity Pro Sport.** The research notes record Velocity as designer-led with no self-serve
  tool; the research report says Velocity has a kit builder that prices a leotard from the
  complexity of its design (citing a different page, `gymnasticsleotards.co.uk/pages/clubs`).
  Neither was operated.[^report] **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **[INFERRED]** Milano's exit removes a major UK supplier for the 2026-27 season; UK club demand
  likely shifts to Quatro and overseas brands.[^notes-styles]
