# Question

The owner said coaches "select from pre-made assets or designs but still allow for significant
customization". Someone must author those designs.

# Options

1. **Designers in the same product** publish templates and choose which parts a coach may change
   (the designer view doubles as the template editor).
2. **The software vendor** ships a fixed library.
3. **A leotard maker** uses the tool in-house and gives its club customers the coach view of its
   own catalogue (the model GK, Sylvia P and Quatro run with their web studios).

# Recommendation **[INFERRED]**

Option 1, which also serves option 3: the same template mechanism lets a maker publish its
catalogue. It implies a "lock/unlock" layer on every design parameter.
